Dark chapters are not harder melee. They are melee you cannot see. Put light on the room, then parry. A swing in an unlit annex is how a Charm run becomes a death run.

The Minoan sphere is the tool for a light gate. It reopens a path. It is not a damage farm. If a lens prompt never appears in Chapter III, that is the October 6 interaction fix, not a new puzzle. Retest on [the lens page](/resonance-chapter-3-leni-lens-fix/).

## Before the fight

- Light the landing, then the flank. Night fights punish greed more than they punish a slow parry.
- Side rooms off a dark path are collectible checks. Sweep them after the light is stable, not during a swing.
- Do not learn a new weapon mastery in Chapter XIV. The book after the credits is a retest, not a training room.

Collectible totals stay on the [collectibles hub](/collectibles/). This page is only the order: light, then steel.

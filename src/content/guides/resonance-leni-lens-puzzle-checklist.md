If you are stuck at the **Chapter III lens interaction with Leni**, the fix is already out. The October 6 bug-fix notes say that Leni's lens interaction in Chapter III might not trigger, and that this case is fixed. If you left the room because nothing happened, the trigger is what to check, not a chest you missed in a hallway. Install the update, load the room, and try the lens again before replaying the chapter from the start.

## What the fix covers

The listed problem is narrow: in Chapter III, an interaction involving Leni and her lens could fail to start. The notes do not print a beam sequence, a reward, a map pin, or exact controls, so this page does not invent one.

That distinction matters. When the interaction never begins, there is no active puzzle state to solve. Moving beams or searching every corridor will not help a trigger that never fired. Verify the build and the room first; only once Leni's interaction starts does the visible puzzle become the next step.

The Minoan sphere is still the light-puzzle tool; Steam describes Sophia using the stolen sphere to manipulate light. A lens that would not start is not a sign the sphere system changed. It is one interaction in one chapter that failed to hook.

## Check it in this order

1. **Install the October 6 update or a later build.** The fix cannot help an older installation, and the October list is the long one.
2. **Load a save in Chapter III, in or just before the lens room.** Do not skip ahead and hope a flag repairs itself; the notes do not promise a retroactive trigger.
3. **Approach the interaction again** and use the prompt your current build shows.
4. **If it starts, follow what the game presents.** Do not import a beam order from a video recorded during the failure; that video shows the bug.
5. **If it still does not start, confirm the installed build and reload once.** One reload on the patched build is the test. Ten reloads on the launch build only reproduce the bug the patch closed.

The result is a simple yes or no: does Leni's interaction now begin? If yes, play the puzzle. If no, check the version again before restarting the chapter.

## Reading the room once it triggers

Inspect what is visible: the active light source, the surfaces or devices the game highlights, and how they respond to your action. Change one thing at a time so you know what made the scene advance. If nothing responds, return to the prompt and make sure you are using the intended object rather than a nearby prop.

Chapter 3 is the Artefact chapter: it holds **six Artefacts and four Charms**, more Artefacts than any other chapter, and walkthrough notes say it has no combat at all. Once the lens works, there is no reason to rush. The [collectibles by chapter table](/plague-tale-legacy-collectibles-by-chapter/) has the full counts.

## If it still fails

- Confirm the update actually installed, not just downloaded.
- Confirm the save is in Chapter III, near the Leni interaction.
- Reload once on the updated build and try again.
- Note whether the prompt appears, whether the animation starts, and what happens next.
- If it still fails, report it through the game's support or community channels with the build, platform, and chapter.

Do not delete a save or restart the campaign over this alone. The notes do not say old saves are invalid, and they do not promise that replaying from the beginning repairs every case.

## Other fixes in the same update

The October 6 notes list several separate problems. They are not this room:

- **Chapter IX:** crouch could fail in a petrified area after backtracking in the palace. See the [Chapter IX backtracking guide](/resonance-chapter-nine-backtracking-plan/).
- **Chapter XIII:** progression could not be saved after an earlier corrupted squeeze-through.
- **Chapter XIV:** the Book could become unusable after the credits. See [the book after the credits](/resonance-book-after-credits/).
- **The Falchion** could disappear after a DLC install. A missing sword makes everything feel broken; open the inventory, and if the blade is gone, see [the Falchion fix](/resonance-falchion-after-dlc/) before replaying Chapter III.
- **Enemies ignoring you after a restart** were also fixed. If the lens works but a guard stands still after a reload, that is the reaction bug, not the puzzle.
- **Ledge shoves:** enemies who survived being pushed off an edge should now die. That is a combat fix, not a puzzle solution.

## Common time-wasters

- **Replaying before updating.** Reproducing the old failure on an older build tells you nothing.
- **Searching the room before the trigger starts.** The puzzle cannot advance until the interaction begins.
- **Following a generic beam order.** Light puzzles share a theme, not a solution. See the [Minoan sphere guide](/minoan-sphere-light-puzzle-guide/) for how the sphere works in general.
- **Changing several things at once.** You lose track of what made the scene respond.
- **Calling a failed trigger a missing collectible.** The fix is about an interaction, not an Artefact, Blade, Charm, or achievement.

## Quick answer

Install the October 6 bug-fix update or newer, load the Chapter III room, and try Leni's lens once. The update says the trigger failure is fixed. Once it starts, follow the game's prompt. If it still fails, confirm the build and note what happens before restarting the chapter.

The October 6 bug-fix notes list a Chapter XIV problem: the Book was not usable after the credits. That is a post-game tool bug, not a combat change, not a missing weapon, and not a reason to rebind every input. Install the update, open the Book once after the credits, and if it opens, the listed bug is gone. If it does not, confirm your game version before replaying the ending.

## How to check it

1. Make sure the October 6 update (or later) is installed.
2. Load your post-credits save. Chapter XIV, What Remains, is the epilogue, so this only applies once the story is finished.
3. Open the Book and turn one page.
4. If it works, you are done. If it does not, check the installed version and try once more before reloading older saves.

Use the Book for what the game shows you. A video recorded on the launch build that shows "the Book does nothing" is showing the bug.

## If the Book opens but the controls are wrong

A separate line in the same update fixed **Book navigation inputs that could not be modified**. If the Book opens but the page controls are not where you want them, rebind Book navigation only and leave Focus Attack and Prism alone until a page turns. The update also fixed the joystick-swap preference not being saved after a relaunch, so if the sticks feel reversed, relaunch once after setting it. The [Focus Attack and Prism controls guide](/resonance-focus-prism-input-planning/) covers all four control fixes.

## Bugs that are not this one

- **The Falchion** could disappear from the inventory after a DLC install. A missing sword and a dead Book can happen to the same player and still be two bugs. See [the Falchion fix](/resonance-falchion-after-dlc/).
- **Chapter III:** Leni's lens interaction could fail to trigger. See the [lens checklist](/resonance-leni-lens-puzzle-checklist/).
- **Chapter IX:** crouch could fail in a petrified area after a palace backtrack. See the [Chapter IX guide](/resonance-chapter-nine-backtracking-plan/).
- **Chapter XIII:** progression could not be saved after a corrupted squeeze-through.

If you have not reached the credits, you are not in this bug; finish the route first. All the chapter fixes are listed together in the [October 6 update guide](/resonance-plague-tale-legacy-october-6-update/).

## After the credits

Once the story is done, Story Select opens and lets you replay any chapter for missed Blades, Artefacts, Charms, and one-off achievements. Resonance Points are the exception and do not carry through Story Select. Chapter 14 itself holds no collectibles. The [Story Select cleanup guide](/story-select-cleanup/) explains the replay order.

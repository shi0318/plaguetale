The bug-fix list says the book was not usable in Chapter XIV after the credits. That is a post-game tool bug. It is not a combat nerf, not a missing Falchion, and not a reason to rebind every input. Update. Open the book once after the credits on the current build. If it opens, the listed bug is gone. If it does not, confirm the version before you replay the ending.

On **October 25, 2026** the US page was **$39.99**, Very Positive, **1,655** English reviews, **90%** of **475** in 30 days. A stuck book in a recommended game is still a stuck book. The notes marked it fixed. The percentage will not open it.

## Do not mix the book with the sword

The Falchion could disappear from inventory after a DLC install. That is an inventory line, also marked fixed. A missing sword and a dead book can happen to the same player and still be two bugs. Open the inventory. If the blade is back, equip it and swing once. Then open the book. If the book is the only failure, do not spend an hour in the bag.

Book navigation inputs could not be modified before this patch. If the book opens and the page keys are wrong, rebind book navigation only. Leave Focus Attack and Prism alone until the book turns a page. One control. One test. Joystick swap that resets after a relaunch is a fourth line. Relaunch once after you set it. Do not blame the credits.

## What the book is not

It is not the Chapter III lens. It is not the Chapter IX crouch. It is not a ledge shove. If you are before the credits, you are not in this bug. Finish the route. The book line is after. Players who "fix" a mid-game lens by hunting a post-credits menu are in the wrong chapter. Read the number.

It is not a discount. **$39.99** on this check is the sticker. A working book does not change it. A broken book on an unpatched install does not mean the game is unsellable. It means you have not installed the list that named the fix. Very Positive at 90% of 475 includes people who never opened the book and people who did after the patch. Join the second group if you care about the tool.

## After it opens

Use it for what the game shows, not for a route imported from a video whose whole point was "the book does nothing." That video is the bug. The patch is the opposite. Then go back to the fight you actually meant to learn. The parry still starts by facing the guard. The sling is still for the light. The book is a tool after the credits, not a substitute for either.

**75 GB** on an SSD remains the disk line. A hitch while the book opens is a load. Move the install if you are on a hard drive, then open it again. **36** achievements. If one names the book, follow the in-game text after the book works. Do not grind the credits on the launch build hoping a pop will force the menu. Update, open the book, turn one page, stop.

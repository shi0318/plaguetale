Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy is a linear adventure that mixes combat, stealth, puzzles, and exploration. Steam reviewers describe that blend, and the official description backs it: Sophia fights in dynamic melee, but against the creature behind the myth she can only hide, outsmart it, and push forward. The useful question is not which style to pick for the whole game, but which one a given scene asks for.

## The short answer

Fight people, hide from the creature. Human enemies with weapons are built around the parry system covered in the [combat guide](/combat/). The creature that hunts Sophia from Chapter 6 onward cannot be killed; every encounter with it is a chase or a stealth section. Reviewers also describe the story as linear, so this is a choice you make scene by scene inside a directed campaign, not a branching route or a build that changes the plot.

## Why this game plays differently from Innocence and Requiem

The earlier games trained players to sneak: stealth, distraction, a sling, alchemical ammunition, and fire and light used to steer rat swarms. Resonance keeps the hiding but moves the centre of gravity to melee. Its Steam text promises violent close combat with agility, tricks, sharp reflexes, parries, and powerful strikes, and launch reviews describe the combat as the main change. Players who wanted the older pair of protagonists, Amicia and Hugo, are describing taste rather than a missing system: this story follows Sophia, and her toolset is built for fighting people. Expect to draw a sword far more often than in the earlier games, and to hide mainly when the creature or the script demands it.

## Do not stealth a street fight

A carnival alley with two guards is a fight. Crouch-walking it because the earlier games let you avoid rats hands the second guard a free swing. There are no rat swarms to kite in Resonance; if a guide tells you to sneak past a horde of rats, it is describing a different Plague Tale game.

What still counts as stealth:

- A scripted hide during Presence pressure. That is a chapter instruction, not a combat style.
- The creature chases from Chapter 6 onward.
- A side room you slip into for a Charm and then leave. That is a collectible detour, not a patrol route.

Skill points spent on a pure avoid-build will not create a silent takedown the campaign does not have.

## How to clear a two-guard street

1. Parry the visible swing.
2. Step so the second guard is not behind you before you punish.
3. If a second guard flanks, turn to face them and parry; do not start anything slow with a sword already in range.
4. Use the sphere only after the steel is down, or when the door is a light gate.
5. Collect the Charm in the side room last. Sweeping it mid-swing is how a collectible run becomes a reload.

The flank in detail is on the [combat guide](/combat/).

## Surviving the creature

Walkthrough coverage of the late chapters gives a consistent set of rules for the creature sections:

- It tracks the **blue trail** that sprinting leaves on the floor. Walk or crouch in dark areas; running is what gets you caught.
- Lit areas and braziers are safe, and rubble is cover when it breathes in.
- It **breathes out to kill braziers**, so stay behind cover when it does.
- After Chapter 10, Light Concentrate lets you burn it back at a brazier to buy ground.

The sphere powers behind those rules are on the [Minoan sphere guide](/minoan-sphere-light-puzzle-guide/).

## Light the room before you swing

Dark chapters are not harder melee; they are melee you cannot see. Clear a dark room in this order:

1. Light the landing, then the flank.
2. Parry what you can now see.
3. Sweep side rooms and cliff Resonance Points after the fight, not during it.

Night fights punish greed more than they punish a slow parry. Before Chapter 10 the Minoan sphere opens light gates and reopens paths; it is not a damage farm. Collectible totals per chapter are on [collectibles by chapter](/plague-tale-legacy-collectibles-by-chapter/).

## When a stealth line fails because of a bug

Some "stealth failures" were named bugs. The October 6 bug-fix update fixed crouch failing in a petrified area of Chapter IX after backtracking in the palace, and fixed Leni's lens interaction in Chapter III not triggering. If either blocked you, install the update and reload that save before replaying the chapter; the [October 6 update guide](/resonance-plague-tale-legacy-october-6-update/) and [Chapter 9 crouch guide](/resonance-chapter-nine-backtracking-plan/) cover both.

## Late chapters: do not experiment

Chapter XIV is short if you already understand the parry. Do not learn a new weapon mastery there; the book interaction after the credits is a fix to check on the updated build, not a training room. The [book after the credits](/resonance-book-after-credits/) page covers it.

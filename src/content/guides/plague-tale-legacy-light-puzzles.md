The store page says Sophia uses a stolen **Minoan sphere** to manipulate light — a force **Daedalus** wove into the island. That is the puzzle spine of **Resonance**.

![Light puzzle](/images/ptl-light-puzzle.webp)

Hidden achievements named Resonance, Patera, Refraction, and Fading Light cluster around light-and-sound language. Valve hides those requirements, so this page does not assign them to rooms.

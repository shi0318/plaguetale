Carnival alleys in **Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy** are built for two bodies, not one duel. Parry the guard in front of you. The second guard is the flank. If you wind a sling shot while the first sword is still in range, the flank arrives for free.

This is not a rat horde and not a stealth alley. Hide only when the Presence script says hide. The longer split is [do not stealth the street](/resonance-do-not-stealth-the-street/).

## The room

1. Parry the visible swing. [Parry basics](/combat-parry-basics/).
2. Step so the second guard is not behind you before you punish.
3. Use the sling or the sphere only after the steel is down, or when the door is a light gate. [Parry, then sling](/resonance-parry-then-sling/).

A Charm in a side room can wait until both guards are down. Sweeping it mid-swing is how a collectible run becomes a reload.

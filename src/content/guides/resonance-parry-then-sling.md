**Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy** is a melee game with a sling, not a stealth game that sometimes swings. Parry the guard in front of you. Use the sling when a light gate or a distant target is the actual problem. Hiding is for the script that demands it, not for every street.

Tight streets punish a tunnel vision on one guard. If a second body flanks, finish the parry or disengage. Do not wind a sling shot with a sword already in range.

## A room, in order

1. Parry the swing you can see. The [parry basics](/combat-parry-basics/) page is that input.
2. If the room is gated by light, switch to the sphere or the sling. That is a puzzle beat, not a damage race. See [sling and melee](/plague-tale-legacy-sling-and-melee/).
3. Night chapters: light the space before you commit. A dark annex is a collectible check, not a place to experiment with a new mastery.

There are no rat swarms to kite. If a guide tells you to sneak past a horde of rats, it is describing a different Plague Tale game.

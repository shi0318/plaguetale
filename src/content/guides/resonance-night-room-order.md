A dark chapter is the same parry game with the lights off. Turn the lights on first. The Minoan sphere opens a light gate. It does not replace the sword, and it is not a damage farm.

If Chapter III never shows Leni's lens prompt, that is the October 6 fix, not a new solution. Retest on [the lens page](/resonance-chapter-3-leni-lens-fix/) before you replay the chapter.

## Order

1. Light the landing and the flank. [Light before the swing](/resonance-light-before-the-swing/).
2. Parry what you can now see.
3. Side rooms and cliff Resonant Points come after the fight, not during it. Totals stay on [collectibles](/collectibles/).

Do not learn a new mastery in the last chapter. Chapter XIV is a short ending if you already understand the parry.

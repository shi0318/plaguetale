**Recent Steam player reports say that collectibles in Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy persist after death.** This is a community observation from reviews checked on August 28, 2026, not an official save-system specification and not a claim that this site tested the behavior.

## Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy death answer

The practical takeaway is reassuring: if a reported collectible has been registered before a death, players say it remains counted afterward. That separates collectible tracking from other forms of progress. It does not mean every nearby action, puzzle state, or skill-related change is preserved.

Because the evidence is report-based, read “persist” narrowly. It describes the collectible behavior players reported, not a guarantee about every collectible type, every checkpoint, or every possible interruption.

## Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy collectibles and progress

Community reports distinguish collectibles from skill points: collectibles are said to survive death, while skill points are said not to. That difference matters when deciding what to prioritize before taking a risk. A player can treat a registered collectible as less urgent to repeat, but should not assume the same rule applies to unregistered discoveries or unrelated progression.

The supplied reports do not describe a complete save timeline. They do not say how the game names each collectible, whether all categories behave identically, or how the count is displayed. This guide therefore avoids inventing an inventory layout or a universal checkpoint rule.

## Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy cleanup use

For cleanup, note what you believe has already registered, then use the released game’s own progress screen or Story Select report as your reference. If a collectible still appears missing, revisit the relevant story section rather than assuming death erased it. Exact chapter placements and routes are outside the evidence available for this page.

Do not reset a run solely because a death occurred after a collectible report. The community evidence suggests that the collectible itself should remain, while it gives no matching guarantee for skill points.

## Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy report status

This is intentionally labelled **a player report**. Recent Steam reviews checked on August 28, 2026 are not the same as an official Asobo or Focus explanation, and this site makes no first-hand claim.

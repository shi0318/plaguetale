The bug-fix notes name Chapter IX: you might not have been able to crouch in a petrified area after backtracking in the palace. If a stealth line failed because crouch would not take, that was this bug, not a sudden decision that the room is a brawl. Install the update. Walk back. Crouch once. Then decide.

Resonance launched **August 27, 2026**. On **October 26, 2026** the US page was **$39.99**, Very Positive, **1,655** English reviews, **90%** of **475** in 30 days. A stuck crouch in a well-reviewed game is still a stuck crouch. The notes marked it fixed. An August install will still refuse.

## The retest

Load a save at the palace backtrack, not at a random fight. Enter the petrified area. Crouch. If it takes, the listed bug is gone. Play the stealth line the room wanted. If it does not take, confirm the patch. One reload on the current build is the test. Ten reloads on the launch build reproduce the bug they closed.

Do not drop difficulty because crouch failed. Difficulty is damage. Crouch is an input the patch said it restored. Do not rebind five buttons to fix it. Focus Attack and Prism rebinds were separate lines. Fix the line you are standing in.

Chapter III's lens is not this room. Chapter XIII's save block is not this room. Chapter XIV's book after the credits is not this room. Read the chapter number. A crouch, a lens, a save, and a book are four fixes. You only need the one under your feet.

## If crouch works and the room is still a fight

Then it is a fight. Turn toward the second guard and parry. Do not treat a flank as a stealth fail you can crouch out of after the sword is already in your back. The sling is for the light gate, not for the body you have not faced. A working crouch does not make every palace room optional combat.

Enemies who survived a ledge shove should die if they leave the edge, on this patch. That is a different test. Do not shove a stealth route off a cliff because crouch scared you once on the old build.

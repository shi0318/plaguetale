## Short answer

The antagonist of Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy is **unnamed in the official material**. The Steam store description (App 2713000) describes it as "a restless presence" that "lurks in the shadows," follows the player around, "seemingly knowing your every move," and hunts through the island's depths.

![Shadow creature](/images/ptl-creature-shadow.webp)

## What the official text confirms

- It is tied to the **Minotaur myth** at the heart of a devastating curse — "the terrifying creature hidden behind the myth" resonates with Sophia.
- The official survival instruction is explicit: "You can't stop, only **hide, outsmart, and push forward**."
- The trailer post describes signs of the entity surfacing **across both eras** — Sophia's Middle Age and ancient Minoan times.
- "It is said that only a few came back from the island. … Every step is a risk. Every pause, a potential end."

## What is not published

The announcement does **not** publish the entity's name, health, weaknesses, or encounter order. Searches of the player forums for a confirmed fight guide return reports, not a verified moveset. This page therefore documents the official framing — hide, outsmart, push forward — and does not convert a community video into a boss strategy.

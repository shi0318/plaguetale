The antagonist is **unnamed in the official material**. The Steam store description (App 2713000) calls it "a restless presence" that "lurks in the shadows," follows the player, "seemingly knowing your every move," and hunts through the island's depths.

![Shadow creature](/images/ptl-creature-shadow.webp)

## What the store text says

- It is tied to the **Minotaur myth** at the heart of a devastating curse — "the terrifying creature hidden behind the myth" that will soon resonate with Sophia.
- The survival instruction is explicit: "You can't stop, only **hide, outsmart, and push forward**."
- The trailer post describes signs of the entity surfacing **across both eras** — Sophia's Middle Ages and ancient Minoan times.
- "It is said that only a few came back from the island. … Every step is a risk. Every pause, a potential end."

Asobo has not published its name, health, weaknesses, or encounter order.

## Why players call it the Beast

Steam reviewers settled on **the Beast** as the name for this hunter, and several call it more frightening than the rats of the earlier games. One review put it as: the rats could be scary, but the Beast is worse. The [story guide](/story/) covers how the hunter replaces the swarm as the series' fear engine.

## Do not confuse it with Theseus

Theseus is the hero resonating *within* Sophia — an ally and a second perspective. The entity is the other presence, the one hunting her. Both are linked to the myth, which is why the two are easy to mix up. See the [Theseus page](/characters/theseus/).

## Can you fight it?

According to walkthrough notes on the [all-bosses guide](/plague-tale-legacy-all-bosses/), no. The creature that hunts Sophia from **Chapter 6** onward cannot be killed; every encounter with it is a chase or a stealth section. That matches the official "hide, outsmart, push forward" line.

## How to survive the hunts

Player walkthrough notes describe a consistent set of rules:

- **Stay in the light.** Lit areas and braziers are safe.
- **Do not sprint in the dark.** The creature tracks the **blue trail** that sprinting leaves on the floor. Walk or crouch instead.
- **Watch its breath.** It breathes out to kill braziers — get behind cover when it does. Rubble can work as cover.
- **Use Light Concentrate after Chapter 10.** Once the sphere gains Light Concentrate at Daedalus' Workshop, you can burn the creature at a brazier to push it back and buy ground.

The [Minoan sphere guide](/minoan-sphere-light-puzzle-guide/) explains the sphere powers, and [stealth or combat](/stealth-or-combat/) covers when hiding is the right call elsewhere.

## Is it the Minotaur?

The store text ties it to "the creature hidden behind the myth" without naming it. The final boss in Chapter 13, which walkthrough notes call **Asterion, the Minotaur**, is fought at a Minotaur statue in the heart of the Labyrinth — see the [Asterion page](/characters/asterion/). Whether the hunter and that statue fight are the same being is a story point the official material does not spell out.

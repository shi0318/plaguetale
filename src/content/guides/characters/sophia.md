**Sophia** is the protagonist. The Steam store description introduces her as "a fierce young plunderer, on the run but determined to uncover the secrets of her past," and Asobo's Sophia's Story devblog adds the detail players search for: she is a renowned plunderer and smuggler nicknamed **"The Scorpion of the Seas."**

![Sophia in combat](/images/ptl-sophia-combat.webp)

## How old is Sophia, and when is this?

The game is set **15 years before *A Plague Tale: Requiem***, and Sophia is **22** in the main story. It also journeys back through her childhood in the plunderer camp where she grew up. If you met her in *Requiem* as the independent, pirate-like woman urging Amicia to stop being violent, this is the younger, angrier version of that woman.

## What the official record says about her

- She fights with **dynamic, violent melee** built on agility, tricks, and sharp reflexes — parries and powerful strikes rather than armour.
- Her journey takes her to the **Minotaur's Island**, pursued by an army also drawn to reclaim a mysterious treasure.
- She is guided by knowledge safeguarded in **her notebook**, following in her crew's footsteps.
- She was **cast out by her old mentor**, and defies him in a perilous treasure hunt "that will shape her forever."
- *Requiem* established that she was placed in a convent by her mother's family, escaped, and found her father again. The devblog confirms that **Requiem was not Sophia's first encounter with the Macula**.

## Who plays Sophia?

**Anna Demetriou** returns to the role she voiced in *Requiem*, and this time she performed the motion capture as well as the voice. In the official interview she describes this Sophia as "bloodthirsty, violent and hot headed," with a "screw it" mentality and moments where she is "overcome with rage / out of control." The full interview notes are on [who plays Sophia](/sophia-voice-actor/).

## Family and the camp

The devblog names her father: **Alec**, who led the plunderer camp where she grew up. The camp is a playable setting for the childhood sections. See the [Alec page](/characters/alec/) for what is and is not known about his present-day role.

Her named friend on the island is **Leni**, who helps her face dangerous enemies and deadly trials — see the [Leni page](/characters/leni/). The companions she once tried to escape are, in the release post's words, "never far behind."

## The presence within her

The store description ends on the hook that defines her arc: the creature hidden behind the myth "will soon **resonate** with her." The gameplay announcement later identified what resonates within her as the legendary hero **Theseus**. Sophia's upgrades stay with her: walkthrough notes report that Theseus has his own Focus Attack in the memory sections and that Sophia's skill purchases do not carry over. Read the [Theseus page](/characters/theseus/) for the two-era structure.

## Playing as Sophia

Sophia's side of the game is where the skill tree, the Blades, and most boss fights live. The first boss of her own era is Faro in Chapter 9, and walkthrough notes recommend buying the red-attack parry mastery early for exactly that fight. The [combat guide](/combat/) covers her parry rhythm, the [skills unlock order](/plague-tale-legacy-skills-unlock-order/) covers what to buy first, and the [all-bosses guide](/plague-tale-legacy-all-bosses/) covers each fight.

Reviews that call the story engaging are talking about this cast — Sophia, Leni, Alec, and Theseus — not Amicia and Hugo. The [story guide](/story/) explains how that changes the tone.

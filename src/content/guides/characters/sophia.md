## Short answer

**Sophia** is the named protagonist of Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy. The Steam store description introduces her as "a fierce young plunderer, on the run but determined to uncover the secrets of her past," and the official Sophia's Story devblog adds the detail players search for: she is a renowned plunderer and smuggler nicknamed **"The Scorpion of the Seas."**

![Sophia in combat](/images/ptl-sophia-combat.webp)

## What the official record confirms

- Set **15 years before A Plague Tale: Requiem**, Sophia is 22 in the main story; the game also journeys through her childhood in the plunderer camp.
- She leads a **dynamic, violent melee combat** style built on agility, tricks and sharp reflexes — parries and powerful strikes, not armor.
- Her journey takes her to the **Minotaur's Island**, pursued by an army also drawn to reclaim a mysterious treasure.
- She is guided by knowledge safeguarded in **her notebook**, following in her crew's footsteps.
- She was **cast out by her old mentor** and defies him in a perilous treasure hunt "that will shape her forever."
- *Requiem* established she was placed in a convent by her mother's family, escaped, and found her father again. The devblog confirms **Requiem was not Sophia's first encounter with the Macula**.

## Family and the camp

The devblog names her father: **Alec**, who led the plunderer camp where she grew up. His camp is a playable setting for the childhood sections. See the [Alec character page](/characters/alec/) for what is and is not confirmed about his present-day role.

## The presence within her

The store description ends on the hook that defines her arc: the terrifying creature hidden behind the myth "will soon **resonate** with her." The official gameplay announcement later identified what resonates within her as the legendary hero **Theseus** — read the [Theseus page](/characters/theseus/) for the two-era structure this creates.

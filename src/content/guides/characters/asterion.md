**Asterion** is the classical personal name of the Minotaur in Greek myth. Asobo's tracked Steam posts call it the Minotaur rather than Asterion, but player walkthrough notes use the name for the game's final boss.

![Colossal bull statue over a ritual court](/images/ptl-ss-bull-statue.jpg)

## What Asobo's myth post says

DEVBLOG #3, "Into The Myth," retells the legend: Minos breaks a promise to Poseidon; his queen **Pasiphaë** is cursed to give birth to the **Minotaur**, half man and half bull; Minos has **Daedalus** build a labyrinth no one can escape; Athens must send **fourteen youths every year** to feed a beast that has grown savage; **Theseus** volunteers, kills the Minotaur with the help of **Ariadne's** thread, and then abandons her. Asobo's line is that Resonance begins the moment that thread vanishes. The store page ties the game's hunter to "the terrifying creature hidden behind the myth." None of those posts use the name Asterion.

The [Minotaur myth guide](/minotaur-myth-explained/) covers the full retelling and the themes Asobo draws from it.

## The final boss: Asterion, the Minotaur

Walkthrough notes on the [all-bosses guide](/plague-tale-legacy-all-bosses/) list **Asterion, the Minotaur** as the final boss in **Chapter 13, As One**. It is a mechanics fight, not a duel. You stand before the Minotaur statue at the heart of the Labyrinth:

1. Break the cracked heart with the **Grapnel**.
2. Take the light-beam weapon from the device at the far end and shoot the heart.
3. Wounds open on the statue and creatures crawl out — burn them with the beam.
4. When Ariadne tells you to run, zipline to the raised platform and avoid the corruption slams.
5. Grapnel the mouth from both platforms to break it open.
6. After the escape, climb the circular staircase and finish the wounds with the beam.

Nothing here is parried. Keep moving, keep the beam on the wounds, and treat the creatures as a timer rather than an enemy list.

## Is Asterion the creature hunting Sophia?

The official material does not say. The hunter that follows Sophia from Chapter 6 onward cannot be killed and is handled through chases and stealth, while the Chapter 13 fight is against the statue at the Labyrinth's heart. See the [entity page](/characters/the-presence/) for the hunter and how to survive it.

Finishing the story unlocks **A new beginning** (complete all chapters). Once the credits roll, Story Select lets you replay chapters for collectibles — see the [Story Select cleanup guide](/story-select-cleanup/) and the [achievements guide](/achievements-guide/).

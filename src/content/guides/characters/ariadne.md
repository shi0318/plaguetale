**Ariadne** is named in Asobo’s official myth retelling. That is not the same as a confirmed on-screen companion.

![Minoan arena duel](/images/ptl-ss-minoan-arena.jpg)

## What DEVBLOG #3 confirms

Asobo’s **DEVBLOG #3, “Into The Myth”** (Steam news hub, App 2713000) retells the legend in order:

1. Minos breaks a promise to Poseidon.
2. The Minotaur is born.
3. Daedalus builds the labyrinth.
4. Theseus volunteers.
5. **Ariadne**, Minos’s daughter, gives him a thread. He kills the Minotaur and follows the thread out.
6. He abandons her, and she curses him.

Asobo’s line for the game is: **“Resonance begins the moment the thread vanishes, and nothing guarantees the way back…”**

So the official record places Ariadne in the myth the island is built on, and it places the campaign *after* her thread is gone.

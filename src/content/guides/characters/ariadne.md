**Ariadne** is Minos's daughter in Asobo's myth retelling, and players who have finished the game report that you control her in one of its boss fights.

![Minoan arena duel](/images/ptl-ss-minoan-arena.jpg)

## Ariadne in the myth

Asobo's **DEVBLOG #3, "Into The Myth"** (Steam news hub, App 2713000) retells the legend in order:

1. Minos breaks a promise to Poseidon.
2. The Minotaur is born.
3. Daedalus builds the labyrinth.
4. Theseus volunteers.
5. **Ariadne**, Minos's daughter, gives him a thread. He kills the Minotaur and follows the thread out.
6. He abandons her, and she curses him to a life of solitude and misfortune.

Asobo's line for the game is: **"Resonance begins the moment the thread vanishes, and nothing guarantees the way back…"** The campaign starts after her thread is gone. The [Minotaur myth guide](/minotaur-myth-explained/) explains why the studio chose this legend and its themes of family, loyalty, and betrayal.

## Is Ariadne in the game?

The devblog itself does not describe her as an on-screen character. Walkthrough notes from players do: they report that the **Chapter 12, Fading Light** boss, **King Minos**, is fought **as Ariadne**, inside Theseus's memory. She also appears in the final boss sequence, where walkthrough notes say Ariadne tells you when to run from the Minotaur statue's corruption.

## Fighting King Minos as Ariadne

The key rule for her fight is that **Ariadne cannot parry red attacks at all**. Sophia's red-parry mastery does not apply, and neither does anything else from Sophia's skill tree. Walkthrough notes describe two phases:

- **Phase 1:** almost everything is red. Dodge, then punish the heavy axe swing while it is stuck in the ground.
- **Phase 2:** the axe catches fire and leaves burning ground. Keep moving, never fight in a fire trail, and get behind him after a heavy swing.

Keep punishes short and pick each dodge's landing spot before you roll. The full plan is on the [King Minos boss guide](/resonance-king-minos-boss-guide/), and the [all-bosses guide](/plague-tale-legacy-all-bosses/) puts the fight in context with the other five.

## Ariadne and Theseus

The myth makes Ariadne the person Theseus betrayed, and Asobo frames him as a well-off figure who turns to violence to get what he wants. Her fight taking place inside his memory ties the two together. Walkthrough notes add that Theseus and Ariadne each have their own Focus Attacks in the memory sections, separate from Sophia's. See the [Theseus page](/characters/theseus/).

## What is still unpublished

Asobo has not published a personality profile, skill list, or equipment for Ariadne.

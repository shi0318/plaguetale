**Faro** is not named in the Steam store description or Asobo's devblogs, which name Sophia, Leni, Alec, Theseus, and Daedalus. Everything below comes from player walkthrough notes for the released game: Faro is first an opponent, then an ally.

![Close melee in a camp yard](/images/ptl-ss-melee-clash.jpg)

## Who is Faro?

Faro is the first boss of Sophia's own era, fought in the **palace courtyard** in **Chapter 9, Night Has Come**. Walkthrough notes describe him as the fastest human in the game. When the fight ends, **Sophia refuses the killing blow**, and Faro joins her for the next two chapters.

## How to beat Faro

Faro throws long combo chains that interrupt your attacks if you swing into them. The notes on the [all-bosses guide](/plague-tale-legacy-all-bosses/) boil the fight down to three rules:

- Let the combo finish, then parry or dodge the **last** hit and answer with a Critical Strike.
- You must drain his health **twice**.
- This is the fight the red-attack parry mastery pays for.

That last point is why the [skills unlock order](/plague-tale-legacy-skills-unlock-order/) puts **Aegean Deflect** and its red-parry mastery first: every boss from Faro onward throws red attacks in volume. Without the mastery, red attacks must be dodged. The [combat guide](/combat/) explains the colour rule — normal swings parried, yellow attacks countered, red attacks dodged unless you own the mastery.

## Faro as an ally

After Chapter 9, Faro fights alongside Sophia. Walkthrough notes for **Chapter 11, Patera** — a chapter built as one long lens chain — have Faro working the cranks while Sophia positions the light. The [Minoan sphere guide](/minoan-sphere-light-puzzle-guide/) covers those light puzzles.

Having him along changes one achievement. A community achievement guide notes that companions deal damage, so an ally can land the final hit you needed for **Inherited brutality**; it is easier in fights where Leni or Faro is not with you. See the [Inherited brutality and Adaptable guide](/resonance-inherited-brutality-adaptable/).

## What happens to Faro in Patera

Walkthrough notes for the Chapter 11 boss say that fight against **Irene**, the traitor, begins after her dagger finds Faro at the mine exit. The [all-bosses guide](/plague-tale-legacy-all-bosses/) covers the Irene fight's three stages.

## What is not published

Asobo has not published a biography, background, or equipment list for Faro, and he is not the camp leader from Sophia's childhood — DEVBLOG #1 names **Alec** for that role. See the [Alec page](/characters/alec/).

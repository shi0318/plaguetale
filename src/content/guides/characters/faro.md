**Faro** is a name this wiki keeps on the extra roster. That is not the same as a confirmed biography.

![Close melee in a camp yard](/images/ptl-ss-melee-clash.jpg)

## What is on the record

The tracked first-party material for App 2713000 names Sophia, Leni, Alec, Theseus, and Daedalus. It does not describe Faro’s role, personality, skills, or weapons.

DEVBLOG #1 names **Alec** as the leader of the plunderer camp where Sophia grew up. That is the confirmed camp-side person. Faro is not in that sentence.

## What this page will not do

It will not label Faro “muscle on the water,” invent a training relationship with Sophia, or assign a unique relic blade. Those lines had no source when they were published as Steam community reports.

If a first-party post or an in-game dossier later names Faro’s job or gear, this page should cite that line.

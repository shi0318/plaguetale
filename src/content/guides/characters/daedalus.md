**Daedalus** is named in the official Steam store description (App 2713000) as the craftsman behind the game's central puzzle mechanic:

> "Use a stolen Minoan sphere to solve ingenious puzzles by manipulating light — **a force woven by Daedalus into the island's very heart**."

![Light puzzle](/images/ptl-light-puzzle.webp)

## Who was Daedalus?

In Asobo's DEVBLOG #3 retelling of the myth, King Minos commissions the master architect Daedalus to build a labyrinth no one can escape, to hide the Minotaur. Theseus later enters that labyrinth with Ariadne's thread. Resonance begins after the thread vanishes, so Sophia arrives in a maze whose builder is long gone but whose mechanisms still work. The [Minotaur myth guide](/minotaur-myth-explained/) has the full sequence.

## Is Daedalus a character you meet?

The official material presents him as the builder of the island's mechanisms, not as a member of the present-day cast. Whether he appears in flashbacks or dialogue is not stated in the store text or devblogs. What you meet constantly is his work.

## His light in the puzzles

Every light room runs on the force Daedalus wove into the island, and the stolen Minoan sphere is how Sophia borrows it. Walkthrough notes describe the sphere gaining three powers over the campaign:

| Power | Where | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Light Splitting** | The first trial, Chapter 4 | Splits one beam into several receptacles |
| **Light Reveal** | The trials, Chapter 5 onward | Exposes hidden markings such as safe floor plates and wall symbols |
| **Light Concentrate** | Daedalus' Workshop, Chapter 10 | A focused beam you can aim while near a light source |

## Daedalus' Workshop

The workshop in **Chapter 10, Parallel Paths**, is where the sphere gains Light Concentrate, and walkthrough notes treat it as the turning point. From there, light stops being only a door key and becomes a weapon: it burns corruption off bridges and mirrors, kills Petrified Soldiers, and pushes back the creature that hunts Sophia. The [entity page](/characters/the-presence/) covers how that changes the hunts.

## Reading a Daedalus puzzle

Most rooms ask the same three questions: where is the light source, where is the lock, and what is in the way. Sources are braziers, sun shafts, or Receptacles that hold the sphere; locks are golden spheres, medallions, gems on bull pillars, or stelae; obstacles are corruption, barricades, and unaligned lenses. When a room has red, green, and blue gems, the solution is a one-to-one colour match rather than a sequence. Room-by-room help is in the [Minoan sphere light puzzle guide](/minoan-sphere-light-puzzle-guide/).

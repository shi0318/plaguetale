## Short answer

**Daedalus** is referenced by name in the official Steam store description for Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy (App 2713000), as the craftsman whose work defines the game's central puzzle mechanic:

> "Use a stolen Minoan sphere to solve ingenious puzzles by manipulating light — **a force woven by Daedalus into the island's very heart**."

![Light puzzle](/images/ptl-light-puzzle.webp)

## What is confirmed

- Daedalus is named on the official store page.
- He is associated with the light-manipulation puzzle system — the Minoan sphere lets Sophia redirect light through the island.
- He is a figure from Greek myth tied to the Minoan setting, which the game blends with its own Macula lore.

## What kind of appearance this is

The store text names Daedalus as the **craftsman behind the island's mechanisms**, not as a character in the present-day cast. Whether he appears in flashbacks, dialogue, or only as the builder of the puzzles the player solves is not stated in the tracked first-party material. This page documents the lore reference and does not infer an on-screen role.

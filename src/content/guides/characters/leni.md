## Short answer

**Leni** is the one confirmed human ally the publisher has put a name to. From the official release announcement on the Steam news hub for App 2713000:

> "As she uncovers the truth about **her own family's connection to the island**, Sophia can rely on **her friend Leni** to help her face dangerous enemies and deadly trials."

![Island trail](/images/ptl-island-trail.webp)

## What that sentence does and does not say

**Confirmed by the official post:**

- Leni exists and is described as Sophia's *friend*.
- She helps Sophia face "dangerous enemies and deadly trials."

**Not stated anywhere in the tracked announcement:**

- Leni's gender, age, or appearance.
- Whether Leni is playable or an escort-style companion.
- Whether Leni is part of the plunderer crew Sophia came from.
- Whether Leni survives the story.

## The crew are pursuers, not helpers

The same paragraph adds a detail that reframes the trip: "the companions she once tried to escape are **never far behind**." So Sophia's old crew are pursuing her on the island. Leni is singled out as the exception — the one named helper.

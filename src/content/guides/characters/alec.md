**Alec** is Sophia's father. Asobo's DEVBLOG #1, "Sophia's Story," on the Steam news hub (App 2713000) describes the game's structure through him: the main story takes place when Sophia is twenty-two, but the game also journeys through "her childhood in the plunderer camp where she grew up, **led by her father, Alec**."

![Close melee in a camp yard](/images/ptl-ss-melee-clash.jpg)

## Who is Alec?

Alec is the leader of the plunderer camp that raised Sophia. That camp is a **playable setting** for the childhood sections, so you see where she learned the life that later earns her the nickname "The Scorpion of the Seas." Lead writer Cara describes the main timeline as roughly 15 years before *Innocence* and *Requiem*, with the camp years earlier still.

## Where Alec fits in Sophia's backstory

The devblog cites *Requiem*'s existing lore as the team's starting point: Sophia was placed in a convent by her mother's family, escaped, and found her father again. That reunion is the thread Resonance builds on. Sophia's mother and her mother's family appear only through that backstory; neither is named in the tracked material.

The devblog frames the family theme directly: "In her quest for freedom, Sophia will learn to **rely on herself and find the right allies**… To get through an ordeal as destructive as the one Sophia will face, you need to know who truly deserves your trust." The [Minotaur myth guide](/minotaur-myth-explained/) explains how Asobo ties that idea of family, loyalty, and betrayal to the island's legend.

## Alec's Pendant

Alec's name is attached to a pre-order item. The free **Heritage Pack** pre-order DLC contains **the Minoan Sword**, **Alec's Pendant**, and a **30+ page in-game artbook**. The pendant's type and effect are not disclosed. Two of the three items are named after story threads — Sophia's father and the Minoan era — which suggests both matter to the plot. Details of the pack are on the [release date and editions page](/release-date/).

## Is Alec in the present-day story?

That is not confirmed. The devblog places him at the head of the childhood camp, and the pre-order item carries his name, but neither source describes his role in Sophia's adult sections. Treat any claim about his fate as unverified unless it comes from the game itself.

It also helps to keep Alec separate from two other figures in Sophia's life:

- **Her old mentor**, who cast her out and whom she defies in the treasure hunt. The mentor is described but never named.
- **Her old crew**, the companions she once tried to escape, who are "never far behind" her on the island. They are pursuers, not helpers.

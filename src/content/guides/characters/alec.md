## Short answer

**Alec** is Sophia's father. The official DEVBLOG #1 "Sophia's Story" on the Steam news hub (App 2713000) describes the game's structure through him: the main story takes place when Sophia is twenty-two, but the game also journeys through "her childhood in the plunderer camp where she grew up, **led by her father, Alec**."

## What is confirmed

- Alec is named as Sophia's father in the official developer story post.
- He led the plunderer camp where Sophia spent her childhood.
- The camp is a **playable setting** for the childhood sections.
- His name is attached to a pre-order item, which the community reads as a hint that he matters.

## What is not confirmed

Alec is **not confirmed as an on-screen character in the present-day story**. The scope of his appearance in Sophia's adult sections is not disclosed in the tracked devblog, and this page does not infer one from the pre-order item.

## Context: what the team had to work with

The devblog cites *Requiem*'s existing lore as the sum total of its starting point: Sophia was placed in a convent by her mother's family, escaped, and found her father again. The theme these family threads serve, in the writers' own framing: "In her quest for freedom, Sophia will learn to **rely on herself and find the right allies**."

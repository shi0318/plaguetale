## Short answer

**Theseus** is the legendary Minoan hero the official gameplay announcement names as the presence resonating within Sophia. As she goes deeper into the Minotaur's Island, she experiences **flashbacks to the Minoan era**, and the post states that the mysterious presence resonating within her "is none other than **the legendary hero Theseus**, who lived centuries before her time."

![Minoan ruins](/images/ptl-minoan-ruins.webp)

## Two things that are easy to confuse

1. **The presence resonating *within* Sophia is Theseus** — an ally, and the second perspective you play.
2. **The entity hunting her through the island's depths is something else.** The store description calls it a restless presence that "seemingly knows your every move," and the trailer post describes signs of a "terrifying entity" lurking in the shadows across both eras. That is the antagonist, not the ally — see the [entity page](/characters/the-presence/).

## What the official post confirms

- Theseus lived centuries before Sophia's time.
- Players experience further trials "**through the eyes of Theseus**," described as "even more brutal and demanding."
- The two heroes are linked by fate and the Macula's mysterious legacy.

## What is not stated

The announcement **does not publish the exact controls or the switching rules** between Sophia's era and Theseus's era. The store description says the game "shifts between" the two, but the mechanism — whether a section is one perspective or the other, and how transitions are triggered — is not documented in the tracked official material.

The October 6, 2026 **Bug Fixes update** is still the note to have installed before you judge Nemesis-area stutter, a forgotten image setting, or a chapter that will not advance. Later coverage repeats that patch. It does not replace it.

The note names 21:9 support, reduced stutter approaching new Nemesis Areas, PSSR settings that now save on PS5, and AMD Anti-Lag behavior when Frame Generation is disabled. It also names stability, progression, combat, accessibility, animation, audio, and localization fixes.

## Retest, do not restart first

Load the save that failed after the update finishes. Chapter III lens, Chapter IX crouch, Chapter XIII save, and Chapter XIV book are on the [chapter-fix checklist](/resonance-chapter-3-leni-lens-fix/). The wider list stays on the [October 6 page](/resonance-plague-tale-legacy-october-6-update/).

The update does not promise a frame rate. If the same approach still hitches, separate that hitch from a crash before you change every setting at once.

**A story run of Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy takes about 10 hours on Normal, and most finish reports land between 10 and 13 hours.** Players who go for 100% report roughly 15 to 21 hours. These are player figures from Steam reviews rather than an official runtime from Asobo or Focus, but they have stayed consistent from launch week through October.

## Story length on Normal

Reviews checked on August 28, 2026, the day after release, put a typical Normal playthrough at **about 10 hours**. Later reviews in early October repeated that figure, with others landing nearer **13 hours**. Neither group describes a grind: there is no live-service map, no repeatable activity loop, and the story is linear from start to finish.

What stretches a run is how you play the space. Slow puzzle solving, reading the island, and stopping to sweep side rooms for collectibles all add time. Steam lists adjustable difficulty and a way to play without timed input, so pick the setting that keeps the pace comfortable rather than treating 10 hours as a target.

## How long for 100%

Completionist reports sit at roughly **15 to 21 hours**. The extra time goes on four collectible counters (8 Blades, 37 Artefacts, 30 Charms, 200 Resonance Points) and the combat achievements that need a deliberate setup. The collect-alls are rare: on September 1, 2026, **Hoarder** (all Relics of the Present and the Past) stood at **3.2%** of Steam players. Plan a story weekend and a separate 100% weekend rather than trying to do both at once. Most cleanup happens through Story Select after the credits; the [collectibles by chapter guide](/plague-tale-legacy-collectibles-by-chapter/) shows what is left in each chapter.

## How many chapters are there?

The chapter list has **14 entries**, from Chapter 1, Blood Ties, to Chapter 14, What Remains. Early reviews counted about **13 chapters**, which matches the playable story: What Remains is a short epilogue, and the Steam achievements include chapter milestones named after Chapters 1 to 13 only. The full list with each chapter's collectibles is on the [walkthrough](/walkthrough/).

Chapters are uneven. Chapter 3 is a long, combat-free Artefact chapter with six relics; Chapter 8, Resonance, is a dream interlude with no collectibles at all. If you are scheduling sessions, use chapter ends as stopping points rather than assuming equal length.

## How many players have finished it?

The story-completion achievement, **A new beginning** ("Complete all Chapters"), was at **26.3%** of Steam owners on September 1, 2026, five days after launch. By October 17 it had reached **60.5%**. A short campaign with a high finish rate is the pattern you would expect from a game people actually complete rather than abandon.

## A long Sunday session

One recent player file shows **9.2 hours** played on a single Sunday, which is close to the whole story. That is normal for this game. If you plan to play in one sitting:

- Play the story for the island and the characters, not for a collectible total. Leave cleanup for after the credits.
- If hitching in a fight is the reason you quit, install the latest patch before lowering settings. The October 6 bug-fix update named stutter near new Nemesis areas; see the [October 6 update](/resonance-plague-tale-legacy-october-6-update/).
- Combat is melee and parry rather than a sling-first stealth check, and there are no rat swarms to kite. If a camp fight feels slow, the [combat guide](/combat/) is the faster fix.
- Leni is the named friend from the release material, not a second playable character; see [Leni](/characters/leni/).

## Does death or a restart cost time?

Not much. Players report that collected Blades, Artefacts and Charms stay counted after a death, while skill points do not. That fits the Resonance Point rule documented by player guides: points only save at the next checkpoint, so a death sends you back to collect them again. Do not farm a death loop for points, and do not restart the campaign to fix a missed pickup. The [Story Select cleanup guide](/story-select-cleanup/) explains how to reopen a single chapter instead.

## Replaying a chapter

Story Select opens after the credits and loads any finished chapter. That is why a missed achievement is a short replay rather than another 10 hours. The [missable achievements guide](/missable-achievements-guide/) lists the one-off moments, such as the turtle and the carnival puppet show, that players most often walk past.

Official copy sets **Resonance** **15 years before** *Requiem*, with Sophia at **22**. Reviews after launch say the tone is different from the Amicia games and that they loved the change. The island, Theseus, and the Macula thread replace the rat-city structure.

![Resonance header](/images/ptl-header.webp)

A new beginning (**26.3%**) is complete all chapters. That is the story-completion floor in the 1 September 2026 achievement snapshot — about one in four owners.

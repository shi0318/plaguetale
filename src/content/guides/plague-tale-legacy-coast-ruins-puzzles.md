The opening vista is a cliff of carved stone, a tower offshore, and water that reads as a postcard. The rooms under that vista are light puzzles and short fights, in that order more often than people expect.

## How to enter one

- Look for the beam, the mirror, or the brazier before you look for the guard. If the room has a statue and a shaft of light, the sling is a switch.
- A locked door with no enemy is not a bug. Walk back one terrace. These ruins stack vertically. The shot from the cliff is the map: down, then across, then the tower.
- Combat rooms announce themselves with a person holding a weapon. Puzzle rooms announce themselves with architecture. Mixing them up is how people call the puzzles "unclear."

The dedicated [light puzzle tips](/light-puzzle-tips/) cover the beam itself. This page is the coast: read the building before you draw the weapon.

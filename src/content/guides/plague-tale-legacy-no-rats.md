The question that follows this game out of every comment section is the rats. They are not the verb here. Asobo built Resonance as a prequel that tries a different mold: melee, environmental puzzles, and a story set before the swarm fantasy people bought Innocence and Requiem for.

That is a real design choice, not a missing texture. If you load the game looking for a rat corridor, you will call the combat a replacement and be annoyed. If you load it as a shorter action-adventure with a sling, a melee tool, and light puzzles, the recent Very Positive reviews match what you are playing.

## What you do instead

- Parry and melee when a person is in front of you. The [parry page](/combat-parry-basics/) is the timing. This page is the expectation.
- Use light and the environment for the puzzle rooms. The Minoan-looking coast in the shots is a puzzle space, not a horde arena.
- Do not grind. Players who finished it describe a short campaign, on the order of a dozen chapters, with no filler loop. Story Select is how you return. It is not how you level.

The score on Steam in October 2026 is still Very Positive, about 1,956 positive out of 2,120. The complaint that does show up is that it is a different beast from the first two games. That complaint is accurate.

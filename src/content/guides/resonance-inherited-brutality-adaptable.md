## Why these two achievements are still rare
Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy has 36 Steam achievements, and 19 of them are hidden. Most of the visible ones ask for a combat habit rather than a story milestone, and two of those habits remain unusually uncommon more than two months after the August 27 release. On Valve's public achievement page, checked on November 4, 2026, **Inherited brutality** ("Kill 4 enemies simultaneously with a Focus attack") sits at **9.2%** of players and **Adaptable** ("Kill at least 1 enemy with each blade") sits at **9.4%**. For comparison, **A new beginning**, the achievement for completing all chapters, is now at **61.0%**.

That gap is the useful part. Most people who own the game have now finished it, yet fewer than one in ten has either of these combat achievements. Neither one is locked behind skill or difficulty. They are missed because nothing in the story asks for them, and because both reward patience inside fights that most players end as quickly as possible. When this site first logged the list on September 1, the two sat at 4.8% and 4.0%, so they have roughly doubled, but they still trail every story achievement by a wide margin.

Only a handful of entries are rarer right now: **Hoarder** (all Relics of the Present and the Past) at 8.1%, **Excessive force** at 8.3% (Hidden), and **Superstitious** (all Charms) at 8.5%. **In the name of my kin** (all Theseus' Echoes) is just above them at 9.6%. If you are working toward a full list, these two combat entries are the only ones near the bottom that do not depend on a collectible sweep, so they are worth planning before you start a cleanup run through [Story Select](/how-to-use-story-select/).

## Inherited brutality: what the requirement actually asks
The wording is specific. You need four kills, they need to happen together, and they need to come from a Focus attack. A Focus attack that kills three enemies and leaves a fourth standing does not count, and four separate Focus kills spread across a fight do not count either. Everything about the setup follows from that one sentence.

Focus is not a skill you buy with Resonance Points. As the [skills unlock order guide](/plague-tale-legacy-skills-unlock-order/) explains, Sophia receives it through the story, and the gauge builds up while you fight. That matters for planning: you cannot spend your way to an easier Inherited brutality. What you can control is the state of the room at the moment you release the gauge.

The most detailed public walkthrough of this achievement is the community "Achievements and Collectables" guide on Steam by .• ellovu •. (posted August 31, last updated September 7). Its advice is practical and worth summarising. The author found it hard and recommends two things. First, bring at least four enemies down to a single hit's worth of health before you trigger Focus. Second, wait until at least four of them are standing around Sophia, because the Focus attack spins and hits everyone near her. The more weakened enemies are close when you fire it, the better your odds.

## Set up the room before you spend the gauge
Treat the fight as two phases. In the first phase you are not trying to kill anyone. You are spreading damage across the group so that no single enemy drops early. Switch targets after a hit or two, parry what comes at you, and resist the urge to finish an enemy that is already low. A single stray finishing blow resets your count, because that enemy will not be there for the Focus attack.

In the second phase you let the group close in. Hold position somewhere with room on every side, so that four bodies can actually surround Sophia rather than queuing up in a corridor. Open ground near the middle of an arena tends to work better than a doorway or a ledge, where enemies arrive one at a time. When four weakened enemies are within reach, release the Focus attack and let the spin do the rest.

The same community guide adds a detail that saves a lot of retries: the achievement is easier in fights where Leni or Faro is not with you. Companions also deal damage, and an ally landing the final hit on one of your prepared enemies quietly ruins the setup. Fights where Sophia is alone make it much easier to keep track of everyone's health.

Two smaller habits help as well. Do not start the setup with a full gauge if the fight still has a wave to come, because the enemies you need may not all be on the field yet. And if you are relying on the Pierced Coins bonus described on the skills page, remember that it gives only a chance to keep the gauge, not a guarantee, so do not build the attempt around a second Focus.

## If the attempt does not count
First check the obvious: count the bodies. Many failed attempts come down to one enemy that survived with a sliver of health or one that was already dead before the spin. If the achievement did not pop, the easiest fix is usually to restart the encounter and try again with more patience in the first phase.

If enemies behave strangely after a restart, make sure the game is fully updated. The October 6 bug-fix update fixed "an issue where the enemies will not react to the Player's presence or attacks after a restart," which is exactly the kind of problem that would make a careful setup impossible. The same update also fixed the Focus Attack input not being changeable in the control settings menu. If the default binding feels awkward on keyboard, the [Focus and Prism controls guide](/resonance-focus-prism-input-planning/) walks through remapping it on the current build before you rely on it in a tense fight.

## Adaptable: one kill with every blade
Adaptable is less about execution and more about bookkeeping. The requirement is simply a kill with each blade, but that only becomes possible once you have collected them, and the grave blades are spread across most of the campaign. Our [blade locations page](/plague-tale-legacy-all-blade-locations/) lists eight Blades from the Heroes' Graves and where each one is found. Collecting all of them is a separate achievement, **Plunderer**, which is currently at 17.8%. Adaptable sits at roughly half that rate, even though every player who holds it must have found the blades first, which suggests many people simply forget to swap.

The community guide's advice is to switch blades between fights as you go rather than leaving the whole list for the end. That is the efficient approach. Every time you pick up a new blade, equip it for the next encounter and get at least one kill with it before switching back to your favourite. It costs you nothing, because one kill is all each blade needs, and it means you never have to return to an earlier chapter just to hunt a single weapon kill.

The same author also notes that they believe the progress only counts within one save file. Valve's requirement text does not say how progress is tracked, so the safe plan is to finish Adaptable on the same save where you collected the blades. If you are cleaning up through Story Select after the credits, carry on with that save rather than starting a fresh one.

Valve's wording says "each blade" and does not define whether downloadable swords are included. Do not count on any DLC weapon standing in for a grave blade. Get a kill with every blade you collected from the Heroes' Graves, and treat anything else as optional.

## Combine both achievements in one cleanup pass
These two pair well. If you still need several blade kills, use those kills to soften a group for the Focus attempt: hit the first enemy with one new blade, swap, and finish a different enemy with the next. That turns the patient first phase of an Inherited brutality setup into progress for Adaptable at the same time. Weaker enemies, such as archers, are good targets for your blade-swap kills because they go down quickly and keep the rest of the group intact for the spin.

While you are in those fights, a few other visible achievements share the same habits. **Sharp enough for you?** (25 critical-strike kills) is at 56.9%, and **Untouchable** (six successful parries in a row) is at 25.5%; the [Untouchable guide](/plague-tale-legacy-untouchable-achievement/) covers that one in detail. **Like a warrior**, unlocking all six mastery branches, is at 11.7% and is covered in the [mastery branches guide](/plague-tale-legacy-mastery-branches/).

The full list with every current rate lives in the [achievements guide](/achievements-guide/). Hidden entries there stay marked Hidden, and this page does the same. If you only want the two rare combat achievements, the plan is short: weaken four enemies, let them surround Sophia, then spend the Focus gauge, and swap to each new grave blade as soon as you pick it up.

Checked October 12, 2026: Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy is 84 on Metacritic. Steam is Very Positive — 1,991 positive out of 2,159. The live PC price is $49.99. Thirty-six achievements. Asobo Studio developed it. Focus Entertainment published it. Launch was August 27, 2026.

The two scores agree on the look, the music, and a story that ends when it should. Files at 11–16 hours call it a Tomb Raider / Uncharted mix in the Plague Tale world, and they recommend it even when they still prefer the first two games. The 84 is not “better than Requiem.” The 84 is “this combat and this island hold for the length they last.”

## How to use the number

- If you want rats and Amicia, this is the wrong score. There are no rat swarms. See [no rats](/plague-tale-legacy-no-rats/).
- If 10–13 hours at $49.99 is the hesitation, the [length page](/plague-tale-legacy-13-chapters-length/) is the clock, not Metacritic.
- A bugfix patch already landed for PSSR, stutter, and combat notes. Judge the image after that patch. See [PSSR and stutter](/plague-tale-legacy-pssr-patch/).

A sale does not change the mold break. It changes whether 84 and Very Positive are enough for a weekend.

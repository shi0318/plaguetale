Asobo kept Resonance on Zouna Engine. That is the in-house renderer behind the earlier Plague Tale games, not a Unreal 5 port. The look people praise in Very Positive files — 1,991 of 2,159 — is that engine on the Minotaur’s Island, not a marketplace lighting preset.

A Switch 2 version is a question Asobo has answered as distance, not as a date. The live listing is PC at $49.99, with full controller support, HDR, and Family Sharing. Do not treat an interview about engines as a console SKU.

## What to do with that fact

- If the coast hitches, that is the [PSSR and stutter patch](/plague-tale-legacy-pssr-patch/), not a reason to assume Unreal would have fixed it.
- If you are here for the look, the 84 Metacritic and the Sunday-length run are the same product. See [Metacritic 84](/plague-tale-legacy-metacritic-84/).
- Engine talk does not change the mold break. No rats. More melee. Sophia, not Amicia.

Judge the image after the bugfix patch. Judge the story after 10 hours. The engine note is why the island looks like a Plague Tale game even without the rats.

The October 6 bug-fix update for **Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy** is a combat patch as much as a technical one. Asobo's notes list enemies who could survive a push off an edge, enemies who stopped reacting after a restart, a "New skill available" popup that could fail to appear, and numerous collision fixes in specific fights. If a fight from launch week still feels wrong, install the update before you rewrite your parry timing.

## The combat fixes

| Fix in the notes | What it means in a fight |
| --- | --- |
| Enemies could survive after being pushed off an edge | A shove that used to be a coin flip should now kill if the enemy actually leaves the ledge |
| Enemies could stop reacting to your presence or attacks after a restart | A guard standing idle after a reload was the bug, not a stealth opening |
| "New skill available" popup could fail to appear after earning Resonance Points from combat | The points may have been there all along; the banner was the bug |
| Numerous minor collision fixes in specific fights | A swing that passed through a shoulder may now connect |
| Improved NPC behaviour in multiple chapters, and improved Nemesis navigation | A pursuer taking a new line is not automatically a new attack pattern |

None of these lines publish a new damage number. The parry is still the parry. What changed is whether the enemy is allowed to ignore the result. The flank rule from the [combat guide](/combat/) still applies: turn to face the second guard, then parry.

## The ledge shove

Find a ledge you already know, with one enemy you can push, and shove. If they die, the survival bug is gone in that spot; stop building plans around survivors. If they live, confirm your version before calling the fix broken, because a launch install still shows the old survival.

When you watch the result, separate two cases. An enemy who lands on a lower platform and stays reachable is not necessarily the bug. An enemy who falls out of the combat space while the fight stays active is closer to what the notes describe. A shove is a combat tool, not a puzzle solution, unless the room asks for one.

## Enemies that ignore you after a restart

This fix concerns behaviour after a restart, so note how you got there: a death, a menu load, or a chapter selection. Move into view, use a clear attack, and wait. If the enemy stays idle, reload the checkpoint once and repeat the same action. If other enemies in the fight react normally, the location and enemy type matter more than a global problem. Do not shove an idle guard off a cliff as a workaround; reload once on the patch and fight them if they react.

## Skill popups and Resonance Points

If the "New skill available" banner does not show after a fight, open the skill menu and check your point total before grinding for more. A missing notification does not mean the reward was lost. Remember that Resonance Points only save when you reach the next checkpoint, so a death before then costs the points, not the popup.

## Control fixes that look like difficulty

Several input bugs were fixed in the same update and are easy to mistake for bad timing:

- The **Focus Attack** input could not be changed in the control settings.
- The **Prism unlock** input could not be changed.
- **Book navigation** inputs could not be modified.
- The **joystick swap** preference was not saved after a relaunch.

If you rebound the parry and Focus Attack stayed on the old key, you were not bad at the fight; the menu was broken. After updating, rebind one action, fight one guard, and confirm it worked before changing anything else. The [Focus Attack and Prism controls guide](/resonance-focus-prism-input-planning/) covers rebinding in detail.

## Chapter fixes that feel like combat bugs

The notes also name specific rooms:

- **Chapter III:** Leni's lens interaction could fail to trigger. A puzzle you "solved" by leaving the room may have been this. See the [lens checklist](/resonance-leni-lens-puzzle-checklist/).
- **Chapter IX:** crouch could fail in a petrified area after backtracking in the palace. If a stealth line failed because crouch would not take, check the [Chapter IX guide](/resonance-chapter-nine-backtracking-plan/) before calling the room a fight.
- **Chapter XIII:** a corrupted squeeze-through could block saving progression. If a death there cost more than the fight, this is the matching line.
- **Chapter XIV:** the Book was not usable after the credits. See [the book after the credits](/resonance-book-after-credits/).
- **The Falchion** could disappear from the inventory after installing the DLC. If the sword is gone, check [the Falchion fix](/resonance-falchion-after-dlc/) before you assume you lost it.

Changing difficulty will not fix a progression trigger. Difficulty changes damage, not whether a scripted interaction fires.

## Stutter that looks like an enemy tell

The same update reduced stutter when approaching a new Nemesis area, fixed 21:9 support, and fixed AMD Anti-Lag not working when Frame Generation was disabled. A hitch at a Nemesis door can feel like the enemy teleported. If the door is smooth after updating, the "teleport" was the hitch. The fix does not lower the PC requirements, including the **75 GB** SSD install; if your machine is under the minimum, the hitch may still be the machine. See the [October PC patch guide](/resonance-october-pc-patch-settings-guide/) and [PC requirements](/pc-system-requirements/).

## Check the fixes in this order

1. Confirm the October 6 update (or later) is installed before loading the save.
2. Shove one enemy off a ledge you know.
3. Restart, load a fight, and see whether the guard reacts.
4. Finish a fight that grants Resonance Points and open the skill menu even if the popup appears.
5. If the Falchion is missing after a DLC install, check the inventory fix before backtracking.
6. Rebind Focus Attack if you need it elsewhere, then parry one flanking guard.

Keep conditions the same as the original problem: same chapter, enemy, difficulty, and approach. If something still fails, record a short clip showing the location, the action, and the result. "Numerous minor fixes" to collisions does not mean every wall in the game is fixed, so tie each result to its build and location.

## Rare combat achievements

The fixes do not hand out rare achievements, but they stop a restart from turning a live guard into scenery. Combat achievements such as **Untouchable** and **Cannon fodder** are covered in the [missable achievements guide](/missable-achievements-guide/) and the [Inherited brutality and Adaptable guide](/resonance-inherited-brutality-adaptable/).

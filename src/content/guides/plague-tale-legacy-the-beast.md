Reviewers of **Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy** keep using **the Beast** for the hunter on the island. One recent line: the rats could be scary, but the Beast is worse. Official store copy still describes a restless presence that knows your move — hide, outsmart, push forward.

![Shadow creature](/images/ptl-creature-shadow.webp)

## Do not confuse it with Theseus

Theseus is the ally resonating inside Sophia. The hunter is the other presence. No official health, name, or phase chart is published. This page does not invent a boss strategy.

The last 30 days on the US Steam page, checked **October 25, 2026**, were **90%** positive out of **497** English reviews. The label was Very Positive. The price was **$49.99**. The all-time English count on the same page was **1,653**, also Very Positive.

That month sits after the bug-fix update. It does not prove every reviewer installed it. A stuck lens, a missing Falchion, or a hitch at a Nemesis door can still be the August build. Check the version before you add your review to the 497.

## What the month is praising

The island, the look, and a combat system more people liked than the worried previews expected. Ninety percent of 497 is not a split. It is a recommendation. Players who wanted the earlier games' stealth story can still bounce. They are not the center of this page.

The month is not a promise that 21:9 was always fine. The patch fixed that support. Retest the edges after the update. It is not a promise that an HDD will stream a new Nemesis area. The store page still wants an SSD and **75 GB**. A hitch on the wrong disk is a spec miss, not a vote against the 90%.

Focus Attack, book navigation, and Prism unlock rebinds were broken and then marked fixed. If your pad felt random, rebind one control on the current build and swing once. Do not drop difficulty because a button did not stick on the launch install.

The sling is still for the light gate, not for the flank you have not turned toward. Very Positive players still have to do that. The percentage only says most of them thought it was worth it.

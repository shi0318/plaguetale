## What $49.99 is

The Windows game on Steam: Sophia, the island, the parry, the sling, the light puzzle, **36** achievements, and the bug-fix update if you install it. Not a Switch 2 port the store has not listed. Not a waiver of the **75 GB** SSD line. Not a guarantee you will prefer this story to the earlier games.

If you own it, the price question is over. Update. Retest 21:9, the Chapter III lens, and Focus Attack if those were your complaints. The 90% month includes people who did that. An unpatched install is a different product.

If you do not own it, **$49.99** next to Very Positive and a 497-review month at 90% is the ordinary full-price pitch. Buy it if the island is what you want. Wait if a sale is what you want. The page can change. On October 25 it had not.

## What not to add to the cart in your head

Do not add a second platform that is not on the page. Do not add a DLC sword you have not lost yet. The Falchion bug was an inventory fix after a DLC install, not a reason to buy the DLC to "get the weapon back." Update first. Open the bag.

Do not add a difficulty drop as a discount. Story settings do not change the store price. A button that will not rebind is a patch, not a sale.

Two tools cover almost every room. The sling interacts with light, ropes, and distant switches. The melee weapon answers a person who has already seen you. Swapping after the swing starts is how the parry window gets blamed for a planning mistake.

## A simple split

- Architecture in the room: sling first. If a puzzle page and a combat page disagree, believe the architecture.
- A weapon trail: melee and parry. The sling will not stagger a committed swing.
- A mixed room: sling the switch that removes the advantage, then fight the person who is left. Do not try to clear the person in order to "do the puzzle safely" if the puzzle is what removes the person.

The [weapons page](/weapons/) names the upgrades. This page is the order. Heritage Pack cosmetics do not add a third verb.

**Olivier Derivière’s soundtrack for Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy is available to stream and download.** The composer’s Bandcamp edition has 30 tracks, including two versions of “The Land” beyond the opening album track. Black Screen Records lists 25 tracks on its double LP and estimates that the second pressing will ship in January 2027. If you came looking for the music after Focus’s November 23 Steam Awards post, the easiest choice is the digital edition. People buying the record should compare the track lists before ordering.

## Where to listen or buy

- **Stream on your usual service.** Use [Focus Entertainment’s soundtrack link](https://album.link/jm7x44db7r2sh) and choose the full original soundtrack. The composer also released individual singles.
- **Download the album or look up a performer.** The [composer’s Bandcamp album](https://olivierderiviere.bandcamp.com/album/resonance-a-plague-tale-legacy) lists 30 tracks, download formats including FLAC and MP3, and detailed recording credits.
- **Buy a physical copy.** Open [Black Screen Records’ double LP](https://blackscreenrecords.com/products/resonance-a-plague-tale-legacy) and compare the four-side track list, selected variant, shipping estimate and checkout total.

August 17 is the digital album date on Bandcamp. The game itself launched August 27 on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and Windows PC via Steam and Epic Games Store. Focus’s launch announcement also confirms Xbox Game Pass availability at launch and Xbox Play Anywhere support. For present-day subscription access, check your region and plan in the [Xbox listing](https://www.xbox.com/en-us/games/store/resonance-a-plague-tale-legacy/9ppbncxp0096). Subscription access to the game and purchases of the soundtrack are separate offers. [Focus Entertainment launch announcement](https://www.focus-entmt.com/en/news/resonance-a-plague-tale-legacy-is-now-available)

## Digital versus vinyl

The LP includes “The Land,” “Patera,” “The Fight of The Braves,” and both performances of “The Depart.” If you are buying for a specific cue, five titles on Bandcamp are absent from the label’s published LP track list: “Run to The Light,” “Treason,” “Echoes,” “The Land (Bouzouki),” and “The Land (Choir).” The digital listing spells track 25 “The Minautor,” while the label calls its D03 selection “The Minotaur.” Those are the names to search on their respective pages. [Digital track list](https://olivierderiviere.bandcamp.com/album/resonance-a-plague-tale-legacy) · [Vinyl track list](https://blackscreenrecords.com/products/resonance-a-plague-tale-legacy)

Black Screen Records says its first pressing is sold out through its own shop and calls the second pressing identical. As checked November 24, the second pressing was listed for preorder with an estimated January 2027 shipment. The signed print is listed as sold out. Verify the option you selected at checkout and check whether other items in the same order will wait for the preorder, as the label says it holds combined orders. [Black Screen Records release and shipping notes](https://blackscreenrecords.com/products/resonance-a-plague-tale-legacy)

## A spoiler-light first listen

1. **Start with “The Land.”** It opens the album and was also released as a single. Then compare the Bouzouki and Choir versions at the end of the Bandcamp album. Listen for what becomes easier to hear when the arrangement foregrounds strings or voices. [Composer’s album](https://olivierderiviere.bandcamp.com/album/resonance-a-plague-tale-legacy)
2. **Follow with “Patera” and “The Fight of The Braves.”** Both were also issued as separate singles, so you can find them by title without browsing the late-album track names. Try “Patera” first when you want to follow the voice, then switch to “The Fight of The Braves” for the shift in pace. [Composer’s discography](https://olivierderiviere.bandcamp.com/)
3. **Compare “The Depart” with “The Depart (Solo)” after you finish the story.** They have separate album entries at 4:15 and 4:09. If you are trying to identify a piece from your own playthrough, note where it starts and what you can hear, then compare the album entries by ear. The track list gives album order and durations. It does not document when every cue begins during play. [Composer’s track list](https://olivierderiviere.bandcamp.com/album/resonance-a-plague-tale-legacy)

## Who you hear in the score

Derivière composed and produced the music. Evgenios Voulgaris is credited with yaylı tambur and rebab, Eric-Maria Couturier with cello, and Giani Caserotto with guitar and bouzouki. In the studio’s July devblog, Derivière explains that he wanted the emotional role associated with Couturier’s cello in earlier games to have a Greek and Ottoman color in this setting. He recorded with Voulgaris in Patras and Paris as well as remotely. [Composer interview on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2713000/view/1839676055882734) · [Album credits](https://olivierderiviere.bandcamp.com/album/resonance-a-plague-tale-legacy)

Vasiliki Anastasiou conducted the Amalgamation Choir in Cyprus and sings as a soloist alongside Giorgos Kalogirou. Both are credited for lyrics adaptation. Derivière describes adapting the words into Cypriot Greek and using recordings of Anastasiou reading them to hear the accents before finishing the music. For an example of how the recording team worked, use the composer’s [behind-the-music choir video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IwKUjw08NLk). The game’s dialogue performance is covered separately in our [Sophia voice and motion-capture guide](/sophia-voice-actor/). [July devblog](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2713000/view/1839676055882734)

## What the Steam Awards post announced

On November 23, Focus asked Steam players to nominate *Resonance* for the **Best Soundtrack Award**. This is a publisher request during the nomination stage. Finalists and winners are announced by Steam in a later stage. To see the current stage, open Valve’s awards page and find Best Soundtrack.

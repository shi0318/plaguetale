**Sophia** is the named lead of **Resonance**. Official story material: she is **22**, the game sits **15 years before Requiem**, and she is nicknamed **the Scorpion of the Seas**. Anna Demetriou returns for the role.

![Sophia in combat](/images/ptl-sophia-combat.webp)

Leni is the named friend. Alec is her father and camp leader. Theseus is the second perspective. Reviews that call the story engaging are talking about this cast, not Amicia.

The first guard is the parry you can see. The second guard is the one who hits you while the sling is winding. Turn until that second body is in front of the camera, then parry. Do not treat a flank as a stealth fail. The street was a fight.

The room order is on [the street page](/resonance-chapter-street-fight/). This page is only the second body.

## The correction

1. Finish or abandon the first parry. A half-finished riposte with a sword in your back is a death.
2. Face the flank. [Parry basics](/combat-parry-basics/).
3. If the door behind them is a light gate, use the sphere after both guards are down. [Parry, then sling](/resonance-parry-then-sling/).
4. A Charm in the side room waits. Sweeping it during the flank is how the attempt ends.

Dying here reloads the fight. It does not refund a skill point. [After death](/what-happens-when-you-die/).

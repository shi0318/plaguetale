A large bugfix update shipped after launch, and the notes lead with the image. PSSR behavior, frame hitches, and an AMD issue where a setting did not stick are on that list. Combat, audio, and localization fixes are on it too.

If the coast looks like it is hitching every time you turn, patch before you drop the resolution and decide the game cannot run. The minimum-spec argument and the PSSR argument are different. One is your GPU. The other was a bug with a note attached.

## After you update

- Re-open the upscaler setting. The AMD note was that a setting failed to apply. Confirm it after the patch, do not trust the old menu state.
- Replay the fight that stuttered, not the whole chapter. Story Select exists for that.
- If audio desync was the complaint, that list is in the same patch. Do not file it as a separate "wait for a hotfix" issue if you have not installed this one.

Performance settings that are actually settings live on the [PC page](/pc-system-requirements/). This page is the patch that made some of those settings meaningful.

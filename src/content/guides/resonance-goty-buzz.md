**Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy** is **Very Positive** on Steam: **1,720** of **1,862** reviews. That is about **92%**. It launched **27 August 2026**.

![Sophia on the island](/images/ptl-hero-banner.webp)

## What players are saying

Recent reviews call it a personal 2026 GOTY so far, praise story and combat, and say it is shockingly stable next to earlier Plague Tale PC launches. Several note a different tone from Innocence and Requiem. The creature — “the Beast” — is called more frightening than the rats.

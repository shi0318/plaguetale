People who rolled credits on Normal describe about 10 hours. Others land near 13 hours and count about 13 chapters. Both reports show up in the same week of reviews. Neither describes a grind.

That length is why the "underrated" comments and the "I finished it on a sale" comments sit next to each other. It is a story you can finish. It is not a live-service map.

## What the length means for collectibles

- Death keeps collectibles in player reports and drops skill points. Do not farm a death loop for points. The [skill page](/skill-points-after-death/) has that split.
- Story Select is the cleanup tool. Missable achievements are a list, not a reason to restart the 10 hours if Select can reopen the chapter.
- A second difficulty is a second 10 hours, not a new game plus with a map editor.

If a guide promises a 30-hour completionist route, it is padding the coast. The island is big in the screenshot and short on the clock.

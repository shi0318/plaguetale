**Resonance** reviews after **27 August 2026** talk about exciting combat, not only hiding from rats. Steam still lists a skilled melee fighter who parries and strikes. Achievements name throwing knives, critical strikes, parries, Echo's Return, and Focus attacks.

![Sophia in combat](/images/ptl-sophia-combat.webp)

Untouchable (**20.3%**) is six parries in a row. Sharp enough for you? (**43.9%**) is 25 critical-strike kills. Those rates show combat challenges are optional pressure, not the story floor.

## Steam Cloud saves

Steam Cloud is listed, so your Steam saves sync through Steam's normal cloud system. The listing does not say how many save slots there are, exactly when a sync happens, or how conflicts are handled.

Good habits when you move between PCs:

1. Let the Steam client finish syncing before you close it or shut down.
2. If Steam shows a conflict prompt, read which copy is newer before you choose. Do not assume an old local copy is the right one.
3. Keep a local backup of an important save; cloud sync is a convenience, not a backup.

Steam Cloud covers the Steam version only. Nothing published confirms cross-save with the PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X|S versions, so do not plan a run around moving a save between platforms.

Cloud saves also have nothing to do with what the game keeps after you die. According to player reports, collectibles you picked up stay counted after a death, but skill points do not. That is in-game progress, covered in the [Story Select and cleanup guide](/story-select-cleanup/), and Steam Cloud neither confirms nor changes it.

## Family Sharing

Steam marks the game as compatible with **Family Sharing**, so it can be played from a shared family library. Steam's own Family Sharing rules decide who is eligible and when the game can be launched; the store tag does not override them.

What Family Sharing does not do:

- It does not add co-op or multiplayer. The game stays single-player.
- It does not give anyone a separate licence or a second copy.
- It does not guarantee that two accounts can play at the same time.
- It does not work across platforms.

The listing also does not explain how separate family accounts handle saves, Steam Cloud, or achievements, so do not expect one player's progress to show up on another account. Before starting, check that the right library and account permissions are active in Steam's Family settings. If access is refused, Steam's message explains why.

## Achievements

The Steam version has **36 achievements**, covering story progress, collectibles, combat challenges, and a few exploration moments. The [achievements guide](/achievements-guide/) lists them all, and the [missable achievements guide](/missable-achievements-guide/) flags the ones you can walk past.

## Controllers and HDR

Full Controller Support means a controller is an intended way to play from start to finish, and DualShock and DualSense pads are both listed. The tag does not confirm remapping, adaptive triggers, haptics, or particular button bindings. The [difficulty and options guide](/difficulty-accessibility/) covers controllers, HDR, and accessibility settings in more detail.

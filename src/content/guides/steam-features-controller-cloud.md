## What the current Steam tags confirm

| Feature | Confirmed scope |
| --- | --- |
| **Single-player** | The Steam version is listed as a single-player game. |

| **Steam Cloud** | Cloud-save support is listed for the Steam version. |

## Do not turn a store tag into an unannounced feature

Steam Cloud is not confirmation of cross-save with PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X|S. Likewise, controller support is not confirmation of remapping, adaptive triggers, haptics, accessibility presets, or a specific controller layout. The listing also does not announce multiplayer, co-op, cross-play, or a console performance mode.

The official FAQ confirms PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S as platforms. Per-platform save transfer, console storefront details, and the final PC-spec sheet remain separate questions and should be treated as unannounced unless Focus or Asobo publish them.

## Before launch

1. Re-check the official Steam listing in your region for any new technical details.
2. If HDR matters to your setup, wait for the final in-game calibration options before assuming a particular brightness or tone-mapping workflow.
3. Use Steam Cloud as a convenience feature, then confirm the live build's save behaviour before relying on it as the only backup.
4. Keep platform-purchase decisions tied to the [platforms guide](/platforms/) and [release-date guide](/release-date/), which track only confirmed availability information.

**Story Select in Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy opens after the credits and lets you replay any chapter to pick up what you missed.** It is the cleanup tool for collectibles and one-off achievements, so a skipped Charm or Artefact never means starting a new file. This guide covers the replay order, what carries over, and what a death actually costs you.

## How to use Story Select

1. **Finish the story.** Story Select is not available as a mid-run chapter jump. If a bug stopped you before the credits, install the October 6 bug-fix update first; it names a Chapter III lens interaction, a Chapter XIII save that could not progress, and the Chapter XIV book becoming unusable after the credits. See the [October 6 update](/resonance-plague-tale-legacy-october-6-update/) and [the book after the credits](/resonance-book-after-credits/).
2. **Open Story Select and load the chapter**, not a new game. The chapter menu shows trackers for Blades, Artefacts and Charms, so you can see which chapter is short before you load it.
3. **Sweep the room you marked.** Blades, Charms and Artefacts are separate counters; check each one.
4. **Quit to the main menu** after a late pickup. Players report that this makes it register immediately.

If a room was ruined by stutter or a display bug, patch first and then replay the chapter. Running the same scene again on a broken setting is how a short game turns into a long evening.

## What carries over and what does not

Blades, Artefacts and Charms all count in Story Select, so the collect-all achievements for those sets can be finished on a replay.

**Resonance Points are the exception.** They save only when you reach the next checkpoint, they do not carry through Story Select, and reloading an earlier chapter resets your total to what it was at that point. There is no tracker for them in the chapter menu, and you cannot farm them on a replay. With 200 in the game and 165 needed for every skill and mastery, sweep them on your first pass. Do not assume the menu grants a point you never collected.

Achievements behave similarly: players report that a replay does not make an earlier action count retroactively. Redo the exact step the achievement names. The one-off moments people miss most, such as the turtle and the carnival puppet show, are covered in the [missable achievements guide](/missable-achievements-guide/).

## Collectibles after death

Steam reviewers reported from launch week that collectibles persist through death: once a Blade, Artefact or Charm has registered, dying does not remove it. That makes a risky fight less costly than it looks. A pickup you had not actually collected before dying is a different case, so check the counter instead of trusting memory.

## Skill points after death

The same early reports say skill points do **not** persist through death. That fits the Resonance Point rule above: points between checkpoints are lost when you die and have to be collected again. Before a dangerous section, reach a checkpoint to lock in what you have, and do not plan to earn points by dying and retrying.

## What happens when you die

A death reloads the fight. It is not a build reset, it does not refund a skill, and it does not complete a collectible for you. Use the retry on the thing that killed you:

1. If it was a street fight, parry the guard you can see, then turn to face the second one. The [stealth or combat guide](/stealth-or-combat/) has the room order for two-guard streets and dark rooms.
2. If the room was dark, light it before you swing; fights in the dark punish greed.
3. If the death came from a stuck prompt rather than an enemy, install the October 6 update and load the same save before starting a new file.

The [combat guide](/combat/) covers parry timing if the same fight keeps ending the run.

## How to find a missed artifact

Start from the numbers. There are **37 Artefacts** (the Relics of the Present and the Past behind the **Hoarder** achievement) and they are not spread evenly: Chapter 3 holds six, Chapter 5 five, Chapters 7 and 10 four each, while Chapters 8 and 14 hold none. Compare the Story Select tracker against the [collectibles by chapter table](/plague-tale-legacy-collectibles-by-chapter/) to find the chapter that is short, then replay only that one.

Your current total is in the Artefacts menu; Blades and Charms are in the Sophia menu below your skills. Chapter 3 is the first one to check: it has the most Artefacts of any chapter and no combat to slow you down.

## Plan cleanup as one short pass

Write down every locked achievement and every chapter that is short on a counter, then group them by chapter. That turns cleanup into a handful of chapter replays rather than a second 10-hour run. The [how long to beat guide](/how-long-to-beat/) has the time estimates for a full 100%.

Resonance sits before the games people already finished. Reviews from series players say the prequel still locks into that story, and they also say the cast is not as easy to love as the later pair. Both can be true.

Play it without requiring the earlier games. The opening does not quiz you on Innocence. Play it after those games if you want the echo, and do not wait for a rat tutorial that is not coming.

The named cast on this site starts at the [character pages](/characters/). The myth layer, including the Minotaur framing some rooms lean on, is context for the ruins, not a boss-rush mode. If you are here for Amicia, you are in the wrong chapter of the series. If you are here for a 10-hour coast with a sling and a sword, you are in the right one.

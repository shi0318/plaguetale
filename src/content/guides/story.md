Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy is an original prequel built around a new lead, a new island, and a myth the studio picks up after its famous ending. The official premise comes from the Steam store description (App 2713000) and Asobo's devblogs on the Steam news hub; the notes on tone and the Beast come from Steam reviews written after the August 27, 2026 launch.

## When does it take place?

Resonance is set **15 years before *A Plague Tale: Requiem***. Sophia is **22** in the main story, and the game also returns to her childhood in the plunderer camp where she grew up. It is a prequel, not a missing sequel: Amicia and Hugo are not the leads here.

You do not need the earlier games to follow it. Asobo describes Resonance as a self-contained experience for newcomers, with surprises for series fans, and the opening does not quiz you on *Innocence*. Series players who post Steam reviews say the prequel still locks into the wider story, though some add that this cast is harder to love than the later pair. Playing it after the other two games gives you the echoes; playing it first costs you nothing essential.

## Who is Sophia?

You play as **Sophia**, a fierce, agile young plunderer on the run and determined to uncover the secrets of her past. She is skilled and strong-willed, and she fights with dynamic, violent melee. The devblog adds that she is a renowned plunderer and smuggler nicknamed **"The Scorpion of the Seas"** — see the [Sophia character page](/characters/sophia/) for her family background and the actor who plays her.

## The Minotaur's Island

Sophia's journey leads her to the **Minotaur's Island**, where she faces deadly trials, navigates treacherous trails, solves puzzles, and stays one step ahead of the army chasing her. Many enemies are drawn to the island, each hoping to reclaim a **mysterious treasure**, and the island itself is a maze that rewards agility, tricks, and sharp reflexes.

A **notebook** safeguards the knowledge that guides her, and she follows in her crew's footsteps as she explores. It is said that only a few ever came back from the island.

## Two eras, two heroes

The game shifts between two time periods:

- **Sophia's Middle Ages** — her present-day journey.
- **Ancient Minoan times** — where echoes of the past shape the battles of the present.

The store text speaks of "the entwined destinies of two heroes linked by fate," connected through the **Macula's mysterious legacy**. Official Steam news identifies the Minoan-era hero as **Theseus** and says players experience trials through his eyes. The [Minotaur myth guide](/minotaur-myth-explained/) explains Asobo's line that Resonance begins the moment Ariadne's thread vanishes, and the [Theseus page](/characters/theseus/) covers his side of the story. The [Macula lore guide](/resonance-macula-asterion-sophia/) follows the November 26 answers about Sophia’s blood, Asterion and Ariadne’s visions.

## Daedalus and the light

Sophia uses a stolen **Minoan sphere** to solve puzzles by **manipulating light** — a force that, per the official text, was **woven by Daedalus into the island's very heart**. The light rooms are the puzzle spine of the game; the [Minoan sphere guide](/minoan-sphere-light-puzzle-guide/) walks through how they work.

## What is the Beast?

The store description calls the antagonist a **restless presence** that lurks in the shadows, follows Sophia, seems to know her every move, and hunts through the island's depths. It is the terrifying creature hidden behind the myth, at the heart of a devastating curse. The official instruction is blunt: *"You can't stop, only hide, outsmart, and push forward."*

Steam reviewers keep calling this hunter **the Beast**, and one recent review put it simply: the rats could be scary, but the Beast is worse. Asobo has not published a name, health bar, or phase chart for it, and walkthrough notes on the [all-bosses page](/plague-tale-legacy-all-bosses/) treat it as a chase and stealth threat rather than a fight.

Do not confuse it with Theseus. Theseus is the ally resonating *within* Sophia; the Beast is the other presence, the one hunting her. The [entity page](/characters/the-presence/) keeps the two apart.

## Are there rat swarms?

No. The rat swarms that defined *Innocence* and *Requiem* are not the core verb here, and that is a deliberate design choice rather than a missing feature. Asobo built Resonance around melee, parries, environmental light puzzles, and a story set before the swarm era.

If you load the game expecting a rat corridor, the combat will feel like a replacement. If you treat it as a shorter action-adventure with a sling, a blade, and light puzzles, the reviews line up with what you play. On October 8, 2026, Steam showed about **1,956 positive reviews out of 2,120**, still Very Positive, and the complaint that keeps surviving is that this is a different beast from the first two games. That complaint is accurate. For how the fighting works instead, see the [combat guide](/combat/).

## How different is the tone from Requiem?

Reviews after launch describe a noticeably different tone from the Amicia games, and many of those reviewers say they loved the change. The island, Theseus, and the Macula thread replace the plague-city structure, and the devblogs frame the story around **family, loyalty, and betrayal** and "a story about dualities." Sophia's performer describes playing her "overcome with rage / out of control," which fits a protagonist who loses control rather than manages it — more on that in [who plays Sophia](/sophia-voice-actor/).

Finishing the story is its own milestone. The **A new beginning** achievement (complete all chapters) sat at **26.3%** in the September 1, 2026 achievement snapshot, so only about one in four owners had seen the ending at that point. The [length guide](/how-long-to-beat/) covers how long a run takes.

## What the story adds up to

Putting the confirmed pieces together, Resonance blends three threads:

- **Combat** — dynamic, violent melee against a pursuing army.
- **Myth** — Minoan legend, the Minotaur, Daedalus, and the Macula.
- **Fate** — two heroes across two eras, bound by destiny.

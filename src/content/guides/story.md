Everything on this page comes from the official Steam store description for **Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy** (App 2713000). Nothing here is speculation — where we do infer from the earlier games, it is clearly labelled.

## When does Resonance take place?

Resonance is an **original story and a prequel**, set **15 years before *A Plague Tale: Requiem***. You do not need to have played the earlier games to follow it, though returning players will recognise the universe.

## Who is Sophia?

You play as **Sophia**, a fierce, agile young plunderer who is on the run but determined to uncover the secrets of her past. She is skilled and strong-willed, and she fights through dynamic, violent melee combat.

## The Minotaur's Island

Sophia's journey leads her to the **Minotaur's Island**, where she:

- Faces deadly trials and navigates treacherous trails
- Solves puzzles
- Fights and stays one step ahead of the army chasing her

Many enemies are drawn to the island, each hoping to reclaim a **mysterious treasure**. Sophia must navigate a treacherous maze, relying on agility, tricks, and sharp reflexes to survive.

## Two eras, two heroes

One of the most distinctive story hooks: the game **shifts between two time periods**.

- **Sophia's Middle Ages** — her present-day journey.
- **Ancient Minoan times** — where echoes of the past shape the battles of the present.

Official text describes **"the entwined destinies of two heroes linked by fate,"** connected through the **Macula's mysterious legacy**. Official Steam news identifies the Minoan-era hero as **Theseus** and says players experience trials through his eyes. The announcement does not publish the final control structure or explain how the two perspectives switch.

## Daedalus and the light

Sophia uses a stolen **Minoan sphere** to solve ingenious puzzles by **manipulating light** — a force that, per official text, was **woven by Daedalus into the island's very heart**. A **notebook** safeguards knowledge that guides her, and she follows in her crew's footsteps to explore the world.

## The presence behind the myth

It is said that only a few ever came back from the island. A **restless presence** lurks in the shadows — it follows Sophia, seemingly knowing her every move, and hunts through the island's depths. This is the **terrifying creature hidden behind the myth** at the heart of a devastating curse.

Early on you cannot simply defeat it. As the official description puts it: *"You can't stop, only hide, outsmart, and push forward."*

## What this means for the game

Putting the confirmed pieces together, Resonance blends:

- **Combat** — dynamic, violent melee against a pursuing army
- **Myth** — Minoan legend, the Minotaur, Daedalus, and the Macula
- **Fate** — two heroes across two eras, bound by destiny

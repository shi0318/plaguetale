**Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy** released on **August 27, 2026** through the official [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2713000/Resonance_A_Plague_Tale_Legacy/) (App ID 2713000). It is the third entry in Asobo Studio's A Plague Tale series and an original prequel rather than a direct sequel. Focus Entertainment publishes it, and the standard edition lists at **$49.99** on Steam.

## Editions and the Heritage Pack

Steam does not list a separate deluxe or collector's edition for the base game. The only add-on is **The Heritage Pack**.

- **Pre-order bonus:** the Resonance FAQ and the release-date announcement both list The Heritage Pack as a free pre-order DLC. The [allies and crew guide](/allies-and-crew/) covers its contents: the Minoan Sword and Alec's Pendant.
- **After launch:** it is listed as its own Steam app, **3837640**, at **$3.99** next to the $49.99 base game.

The pack does not add a chapter or a rat-swarm mode, and it is not the patch. The bug-fix update is free, so install it whether or not you buy the pack. The [Blade locations guide](/plague-tale-legacy-all-blade-locations/) notes that the Minoan Sword does not count toward the Blade achievements.

If you are waiting for a sale, the Very Positive reviews are about the base game. The pack can wait; it does not stretch the roughly 10-hour campaign that Steam reviewers describe.

## What the game is about

Resonance is set **15 years before A Plague Tale: Requiem**. You play as **Sophia**, a fierce young plunderer on the run who is determined to uncover the secrets of her past. Her journey leads to the **Minotaur's Island**, a place of deadly trials, treacherous trails, and ancient puzzles.

For the cast, see the [characters hub](/characters/) and the [story guide](/story/). For combat and the Minoan-sphere light puzzles, see [skills and combat](/skills/).

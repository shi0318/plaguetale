## What we know about the game

Resonance is an original story set **15 years before A Plague Tale: Requiem**. You play as **Sophia**, a fierce young plunderer on the run, determined to uncover the secrets of her past. Her journey leads to the **Minotaur's Island**, a place of deadly trials, treacherous trails, and ancient puzzles.

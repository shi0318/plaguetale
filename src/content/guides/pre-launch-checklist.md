## Release date and confirmed platforms

**Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy released on August 27, 2026.** The official FAQ names:

- PC
- PlayStation 5
- Xbox Series X|S

The tracked first-party sources do not announce a Nintendo Switch or Switch 2 version, and they do not confirm a Game Pass deal. A previous A Plague Tale release is not evidence of a deal or platform for this game.

## PC requirements to check now

Steam currently lists Windows 10/11 64-bit, 16 GB RAM, an SSD, DirectX 12.1, Shader Model 6.6, and 75 GB of available storage. Its minimum target is 1080p on Low at 30 FPS with a Ryzen 3 3100 or Core i3-12100 and an RX 5500 XT or GTX 1060 6 GB. The recommended target is 1080p on Ultra at 60 FPS with a Ryzen 5 5600X or Core i5-12400F and an RX 6700 XT or RTX 3060 Ti 8 GB.

Those are the store’s published targets, not an independent benchmark. Keep the live Steam requirements bookmarked because the listing and patches can change.

## Is there a public demo?

No separate public demo or closed beta is announced in the official sources tracked here. The released PC build is downloaded from the Steam product page. Avoid third-party “demo download” pages: they are not evidence of official access and may distribute unsafe installers.

## What the announced story and combat establish

Steam describes the game as an original prequel set 15 years before **A Plague Tale: Requiem**. Sophia travels to the Minotaur’s Island, where the official description mentions melee combat, parries, powerful strikes, and a Minoan sphere that manipulates light for puzzles.

That is enough to orient a player, but not enough to publish a complete chapter route, enemy health table, or puzzle solution. A reliable walkthrough still needs the exact chapter, build, and observed trigger for each step.

## Launch-day evidence to record

For each puzzle or encounter, record the platform, build version, chapter, trigger, solution steps, and whether a reload changes the result. For PC performance, record the driver, resolution, preset, upscaling mode, and frame-rate result. This prevents an announcement from being mistaken for a tested guide.

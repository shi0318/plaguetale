## Release date and confirmed platforms

**Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy releases August 27, 2026.** The official FAQ names:

- PC
- PlayStation 5
- Xbox Series X|S

The tracked first-party sources do not announce a Nintendo Switch or Switch 2 version, and they do not confirm a Game Pass deal. A previous A Plague Tale release is not evidence of a deal or platform for this game.

## PC requirements to check now

Steam currently lists Windows 10/11 64-bit, 16 GB RAM, an SSD, DirectX 12.1, Shader Model 6.6, and 75 GB of available storage. Its minimum target is 1080p on Low at 30 FPS with a Ryzen 3 3100 or Core i3-12100 and an RX 5500 XT or GTX 1060 6 GB. The recommended target is 1080p on Ultra at 60 FPS with a Ryzen 5 5600X or Core i5-12400F and an RX 6700 XT or RTX 3060 Ti 8 GB.

Those are the store’s published targets, not an independent benchmark. Keep the live Steam requirements bookmarked because the listing can change before launch.

## Is there a public demo?

No public demo or closed beta is announced in the official sources tracked here. The official download route is the Steam product page when the full PC build becomes available. Avoid third-party “demo download” pages: they are not evidence of official access and may distribute unsafe installers.

## What the announced story and combat establish

Steam describes the game as an original prequel set 15 years before **A Plague Tale: Requiem**. Sophia travels to the Minotaur’s Island, where the official description mentions melee combat, parries, powerful strikes, and a Minoan sphere that manipulates light for puzzles.

That is enough to prepare a launch checklist, but not enough to publish a complete chapter route, enemy health table, or puzzle solution. A real walkthrough needs the shipped game, the exact chapter, and the observed trigger for each step.

## Launch-day evidence to record

For each puzzle or encounter, record the platform, build version, chapter, trigger, solution steps, and whether a reload changes the result. For PC performance, record the driver, resolution, preset, upscaling mode, and frame-rate result. This prevents a pre-release announcement from being mistaken for a tested guide.

Asobo kept Resonance on **Zouna Engine** and has talked about Switch 2 as distance, not as a date. The live store listing is still PC at **$49.99**, Very Positive — **1,991 of 2,159** when last checked — with full controller support, HDR, and Family Sharing. Metacritic is **84**. None of that is a Nintendo SKU.

Do not treat an engine interview as a console announcement. A Switch 2 version would be a named storefront, a date, and a performance note. Until those exist, the honest answer is: not listed.

## What that means for a buy

- Play it on the PC / console SKUs that actually shipped on August 27, 2026.
- If the image hitches, that is the [PSSR patch](/plague-tale-legacy-pssr-patch/), not a missing Switch port.
- Engine talk lives on [Zouna Engine](/plague-tale-legacy-zouna-engine/). Length lives on [10 to 13 hours](/plague-tale-legacy-13-chapters-length/).

A sale does not add Switch 2. It changes whether 10 hours at $49.99 feels fair on the hardware you already own.

A hitch when a new **Nemesis** area loads is not a parry window. The bug-fix update for **Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy** says it reduced stutter lag when you approach a new Nemesis area. If the screen froze in September the moment the pursuer's zone started, that line is the one that matches. Retest it on the current install before you decide the fight is unfair.

The game launched **August 27, 2026**. On **October 20, 2026** it was still **$49.99** on Steam, Very Positive at **5,676** positive reviews out of **6,117**. A stutter fix in a well-reviewed game is easy to ignore. It is also the difference between dodging a real swing and dodging a loading spike.

## How to tell the hitch from the attack

Stand at the entrance of a Nemesis area you have already seen. Do not roll as the zone starts. Watch whether the freeze happens before any swing is animated. A loading hitch arrives with the area. An attack arrives with a body.

If the freeze is gone on this patch, the notes did what they said. Play the fight as a fight. Parry the swing you can see. Do not pre-roll the doorway out of habit, because a habit built on a hitch will throw you into the real hit once the hitch is fixed.

If the freeze is still there, check the machine against the store page before you file it as "the patch did nothing." Minimum is Windows 10 or 11, a Ryzen 3 3100 or Core i3-12100, **16 GB** RAM, a 6 GB RX 5500 XT or GTX 1060, **75 GB** on an SSD, and a GPU with DX12.1, aiming at 30 frames in 1080p on Low. Recommended is a Ryzen 5 5600X or i5-12400F and an 8 GB RX 6700 XT or RTX 3060 Ti. The notes reduced the stutter. They did not remove the SSD requirement. An HDD, or a GPU under that line, can still hitch when a new area streams in.

AMD players have a second line in the same update: antilag was not working when frame generation was disabled. If you turned frame generation off and the frame time got worse, that is the antilag bug, not the Nemesis door. Update, then test antilag again. 21:9 support was fixed in the same list. An ultrawide hitch at the edge of the frame is a different bug from the Nemesis approach hitch. Do not stack both complaints into one sentence if only one of them reproduces.

## What else loads when the pursuer loads

The same update improved Nemesis navigation. A pursuer who takes a new path is not automatically faster. Walk the old route once. If the only change is that they no longer clip the corner you used as a safe pocket, that pocket was the navigation bug. Find a new pocket. Do not call it a damage buff.

Enemies who stopped reacting after a restart were also fixed. A Nemesis area you reload into should still have an enemy who notices you. If September's reload left them idle, that idle is not a stealth trick you should keep using. The notes marked it fixed. A restart test is one load, not a new route.

Edge kills were fixed in the same combat block: enemies who survived a push off a ledge should not. If your Nemesis plan was "shove and hope," retest the shove. A plan that depended on a survivor bug is a plan the patch retired.

## A one-door test

1. Install the bug-fix update. The October 6 list is the long one. A launch build will still hitch.
2. Approach one Nemesis area you already know. Do not attack during the approach.
3. If the hitch is gone, fight the swing. If it is not, drop to the Low preset the minimum spec names before you decide the patch failed.
4. Reload the save once. Confirm the enemy still reacts. The restart bug is a separate line.
5. Only then change your route.

Steam lists **36** achievements. **ACH_01** was at **92.9%** on October 20. The rare end of the saved sample sat near **9%**. A hitch at a Nemesis door does not block the first achievement. It blocks the player who dies to a loading spike and calls it a tell. Fix the install, then read the swing.

The island is still a melee game with a sling and a light puzzle, not a loading-screen game. The patch agrees. It spent a line on the stutter so the rest of the fight can be the fight. Use that line.

A second hitch, after the door is smooth, belongs to a different bullet in the same update. Crowd audio in chapter II was improved. German voice lines that could accelerate in chapters IV and IX were improved. A voice that speeds up is not a Nemesis load. A crowd that pops in late is not a parry tell. If you are debugging by ear, separate those. The approach stutter is the one that happens before the body animates. Everything after the body animates is the fight, plus whatever collision fix now lets a shove off the edge count as a kill.

21:9 players should confirm the aspect-ratio fix before they blame the Nemesis door for a stretched frame. An ultrawide bug and a streaming hitch can land in the same second and look like one problem. Set the aspect ratio, approach the door again, and only then decide the stutter line failed. AMD players who disabled frame generation should retest antilag on this build for the same reason. The notes gave that bug its own sentence. Give it its own test.

Sophia's route does not change because a door hitched in September. The light puzzle, the parry, and the sling are still the kit. A hitch that made you roll early taught a bad habit. Unlearn it on a door that now loads. If the door still hitches on a machine that meets the **75 GB** SSD line and the named GPU, then you have a report worth sending. If the machine is under that line, the report is the spec, not the pursuer. The Very Positive score will not load the area for you. The SSD requirement was printed so you would not have to learn that in a boss corridor.

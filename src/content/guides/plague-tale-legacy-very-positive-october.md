Checked October 8, 2026: about 1,956 positive reviews out of 2,120, still Very Positive. That is a month and a half after the August 27 launch, not a review-embargo spike.

The praise clusters on the look, the music, and a story that holds for the 10 hours it lasts. People who played the earlier games often say those two are still the better pair, and then recommend this one anyway as its own thing. That is the useful reading. The score is not "better than Requiem." The score is "worth playing if you want this combat."

The complaint that survives is the mold break. No rats, more melee, characters who are not the pair you already love. If that is your complaint, the score will not convert you. If you have not played the series, the reviews from first-timers are the ones that match your run.

A sale price does not change the shape of the game. It changes whether 10 hours at $49.99 feels fair to you.

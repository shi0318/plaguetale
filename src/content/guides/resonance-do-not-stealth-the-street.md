A carnival alley with two guards is a fight. Parry the first, watch the flank, move. Crouch-walking that alley because the last Plague Tale let you avoid rats will get the second guard's swing for free.

Hide when the Presence script tells you to hide. That is a chapter instruction, not a combat style. The [melee, not stealth](/plague-tale-legacy-melee-not-stealth/) page is the longer version of the same split.

## What still counts as stealth

- A scripted hide during Presence pressure.
- A side room you enter for a Charm, then leave. That is a collectible detour, not a patrol route.
- Chapter IX's crouch, if an older save broke it. That is a bug retest after the October 6 update, not a new stealth system. See [chapter fix](/resonance-chapter-3-leni-lens-fix/).

Skill points spent on a pure avoid-build will not create rat swarms or a silent takedown the campaign does not have.

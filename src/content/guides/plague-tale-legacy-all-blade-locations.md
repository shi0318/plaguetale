There are **eight Blades** in Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy, nine if you own the Heritage Pack. They come from the Heroes' Graves — the bull-pillar crypts tucked off the main route — and they are the collectible set behind two achievements. On Steam, **Plunderer** (collect all Blades) sits at **17.7%** of players.

## All eight Blades

| # | Blade | Chapter | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Rusty Sword** | 1 — Blood Ties | Given automatically during training |
| 2 | **Falchion** | 2 — Felons | Automatic: the barrel by the boat, where you also get the Grapnel |
| 3 | **Kopis** | 4 — From Hell It Rose | After the spike-trap puzzles, through the mirror room: use the pedestal to open the tomb and take the chest below |
| 4 | **Greek Sword** | 5 — Desolation It Wrought | From the four-pedestal room take the O-with-three-lines exit, cross the bridge, drop the cliff, pass the green gate to the camp with the bull pillar, then align the three lights with the pillar's gems |
| 5 | **Sica** | 6 — What Lies Beneath | In "The Architect": first green gate, small left tunnel to the bull pillar room — green light below the pillar, blue below your own, red onto the back-right pillar |
| 6 | **Khopesh** | 7 — Death It Sowed | In "The Whole Damn Army": from the underground exit, head left to the bull pillar and open it with the Sphere |
| 7 | **Broken Spear** | 9 — Night Has Come | In "Hunted": turn left at the start, take the leftmost path, climb the ledge to the bull pillar, then downstairs to the chamber |
| 8 | **Xiphos** | 10 — Parallel Paths | Far-right corner cave: Grapnel up to the bull statue, smash the vases for the blue gem, then align red left, green middle, blue right |

**Xiphos is the last Blade.** Chapters 11 to 14 have none, so if your tracker says 7 after Parallel Paths you have missed one earlier.

## The two achievements

- **Plunderer** — collect all Blades from the Heroes' Graves.
- **Adaptable** — get one kill with each Blade. Equip a different sword before a camp fight and clear the list as you go; this is much faster than doing it after the story.

## Nothing is missable

Blades count in **Story Select** after the credits, and the chapter menu shows how many you found in each one. Quitting to the main menu after picking one up makes it register immediately. Resonance Points are the only collectible that does *not* work this way.

## The Heritage Pack sword

The **Minoan Sword** comes with the $3.99 Heritage Pack. Every third hit lands a powerful strike that stuns. It does **not** count toward Plunderer or Adaptable, so it is a convenience, not a ninth grave blade for the tracker.

## Where to equip them

Blades live in the **Sophia** menu, under your skills, alongside Charms. Swap freely — there is no upgrade tree to lose.

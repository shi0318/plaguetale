There are **eight Blades** in Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy, nine if you own the Heritage Pack. They come from the Heroes' Graves, the bull-pillar crypts tucked off the main route, and they are the collectible set behind two achievements. **Plunderer** ("Collect all Blades from the Heroes' Graves") was unlocked by **9.0%** of Steam players on September 1, 2026 and by **17.7%** on October 17.

## All eight Blades

| # | Blade | Chapter | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Rusty Sword** | 1 — Blood Ties | Given automatically during training |
| 2 | **Falchion** | 2 — Felons | Automatic: the barrel by the boat, where you also get the Grapnel |
| 3 | **Kopis** | 4 — From Hell It Rose | After the spike-trap puzzles, through the mirror room: use the pedestal to open the tomb and take the chest below |
| 4 | **Greek Sword** | 5 — Desolation It Wrought | From the four-pedestal room take the O-with-three-lines exit, cross the bridge, drop the cliff, pass the green gate to the camp with the bull pillar, then align the three lights with the pillar's gems |
| 5 | **Sica** | 6 — What Lies Beneath | In "The Architect": first green gate, small left tunnel to the bull pillar room — green light below the pillar, blue below your own, red onto the back-right pillar |
| 6 | **Khopesh** | 7 — Death It Sowed | In "The Whole Damn Army": from the underground exit, head left to the bull pillar and open it with the Sphere |
| 7 | **Broken Spear** | 9 — Night Has Come | In "Hunted": turn left at the start, take the leftmost path, climb the ledge to the bull pillar, then downstairs to the chamber |
| 8 | **Xiphos** | 10 — Parallel Paths | Far-right corner cave: Grapnel up to the bull statue, smash the vases for the blue gem, then align red left, green middle, blue right |

**Xiphos is the last Blade.** Chapters 11 to 14 have none, so if your tracker says 7 after Parallel Paths you have missed one earlier. Chapters 3 and 8 have none either; the full per-chapter split for every collectible type is on [collectibles by chapter](/plague-tale-legacy-collectibles-by-chapter/).

## How the graves work

Only the first two Blades are handed to you by the story. The other six sit in a tomb or a bull-pillar crypt off the main trail, and four of them (Greek Sword, Sica, Khopesh, Xiphos) open only when you use the Sphere or line up coloured light with the gems. That makes the Blades the set you are least likely to miss by accident, but also the one where a skipped side path costs a whole collectible rather than a single Charm. If a puzzle in a crypt refuses to resolve, the [Minoan sphere guide](/minoan-sphere-light-puzzle-guide/) covers how colours and pedestals behave.

## The two achievements

- **Plunderer**: collect all Blades from the Heroes' Graves.
- **Adaptable**: kill at least one enemy with each blade (4.0% on September 1, 2026). Equip a different sword before a camp fight and clear the list as you go; this is much faster than doing it after the story. The [Inherited brutality and Adaptable guide](/resonance-inherited-brutality-adaptable/) plans both rare combat unlocks together.

## Nothing is missable

Blades count in **Story Select** after the credits, and the chapter menu shows how many you found in each one. Quitting to the main menu after picking one up makes it register immediately. Resonance Points are the only collectible that does *not* work this way. If you do leave a chapter short, the [Story Select cleanup guide](/story-select-cleanup/) explains the replay order.

A simple log helps when you come back: the chapter or scene name the game shows, the landmark near the grave, and whether you took the Blade before leaving. Mark a branch the moment you pass it rather than relying on a screenshot you may not recognise later.

## The Heritage Pack sword

The **Minoan Sword** comes with the $3.99 Heritage Pack. Every third hit lands a powerful strike that stuns. It does **not** count toward Plunderer or Adaptable, so it is a convenience, not a ninth grave blade for the tracker.

If the Falchion vanished from your inventory after installing the DLC, that was a named bug in the October bug-fix notes; see [Falchion missing after the DLC](/resonance-falchion-after-dlc/) before you replay Chapter 2.

## Where to equip them

Blades live in the **Sophia** menu, under your skills, alongside Charms. Swap freely; there is no upgrade tree to lose.

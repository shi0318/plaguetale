Marketing for this series trained people to sneak. Resonance trains you to hit back. Reviews from people who finished it describe dynamic melee, puzzles, and a story, and they mention the combat change as the reason it feels unlike Innocence.

## The loop

- If a guard has a weapon trail, that is a parry. The [parry basics](/combat-parry-basics/) page is the timing. Missing it twice is a heal, not a stealth reset.
- If the room is a light puzzle, stop looking for a takedown. The coast ruins are full of those rooms. A sling is a tool there, not a silenced pistol fantasy.
- Stealth still exists in spots. It is not the fail state. Walking into a fight is allowed. The game is short enough that a wiped scrap does not cost an evening.

People who wanted the older pair of protagonists are describing taste, not a bug. The [character list](/characters/) is who this story actually follows.

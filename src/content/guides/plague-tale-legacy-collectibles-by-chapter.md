## The four counters

| Type | Total | Achievement | Steam rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Blades** (Heroes' Graves) | 8 | Plunderer | 17.7% |
| **Artefacts** (Relics of Past and Present) | 37 | Hoarder | — |
| **Charms** (incl. 5 Pierced Coins) | 30 | Superstitious / In the name of my kin | 9.5% for the Echoes |
| **Resonance Points** | 200 | Like a warrior (165 needed) | 11.5% |

Charm subtypes: Beads, Arm Bands, Bracelets, Trinkets, Seals and Pierced Coins. The five **Pierced Coins** are also the Theseus' Echoes that **In the name of my kin** asks for — at 9.5% they are the rarest collect-all in the game.

**Chapters 8 and 14 hold nothing** — Resonance is a dream interlude and What Remains is the epilogue. **Chapter 3 is the Artefact chapter**: six of them, no combat at all, so there is no excuse for rushing it.

## The one that Story Select cannot fix

Blades, Artefacts and Charms all count in **Story Select** after the credits, and quitting to the main menu makes a late pickup register immediately. The chapter menu even shows trackers for them.

**Resonance Points do not.**

- They save only when you reach the next checkpoint. Die first and you collect them again.
- They do not carry through Story Select, and reloading an earlier chapter resets your total to what it was then.
- There is no tracker for them in the chapter menu.
- You cannot farm them on a replay.

With 200 in the game and 165 needed for every skill and mastery, the margin is **35 points**. Sweep dead ends as you go; do not plan to come back.

## Where to check your progress

- **Artefacts menu** — relics, plus your Resonance Point total in the bottom-left corner.
- **Sophia menu**, below your skills — Blades and Charms.
- **Profile select on boot** — overall completion.

## Two exploration achievements that are not collectibles

- **Sea you later!** (32.0%) — say hello to the turtle.
- **Theater enthusiast** (24.5%) — watch the entire puppet show at the carnival in Chapter 2.

Both are missed by walking past, not by difficulty. Story completion (**A new beginning**) sits at **60.5%**, so around four in ten Steam owners have not finished the campaign at all.

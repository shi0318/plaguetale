Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy has **275 collectible locations** across its 14 chapters. There are four counters and four collect-all achievements, and exactly one of the four behaves differently from the rest. Story completion is not 100%: the full list also includes 36 Steam achievements, several of which ask for a combat setup or a single optional moment rather than a pickup.

## The four counters

| Type | Total | Achievement | Steam rate (Oct 17, 2026) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Blades** (Heroes' Graves) | 8 | Plunderer | 17.7% |
| **Artefacts** (Relics of the Present and the Past) | 37 | Hoarder | — |
| **Charms** (incl. 5 Pierced Coins) | 30 | Superstitious / In the name of my kin | 9.5% for the Echoes |
| **Resonance Points** | 200 | Like a warrior (165 needed) | 11.5% |

Track them as four lists, not one. The achievement names are a good guide to how the game separates them: **Plunderer** for all Blades from the Heroes' Graves, **Hoarder** for all Relics of the Present and the Past, **Superstitious** for all Charms, and **In the name of my kin** for all Theseus' Echoes.

**Chapters 8 and 14 hold nothing**: Resonance is a dream interlude and What Remains is the epilogue. **Chapter 3 is the Artefact chapter**, with six of them and no combat at all, so there is no reason to rush it. For the eight Blades room by room, use the [Blade locations guide](/plague-tale-legacy-all-blade-locations/).

## Charms and Pierced Coins

The 30 Charms come in six subtypes: Beads, Arm Bands, Bracelets, Trinkets, Seals and Pierced Coins. The five **Pierced Coins** double as the **Theseus' Echoes** that *In the name of my kin* asks for, which makes that achievement the rarest collect-all in the game at 9.5%. If you keep your own notes, give the Pierced Coins their own column even though they also count toward Superstitious.

A useful note for a Charm is the scene, the nearest puzzle or landmark, and the exit you took: "near the light puzzle after the water crossing" is easier to act on later than a bare number. Copy the in-game tracker's wording exactly when it is visible.

## Relics of the Present and the Past

The game's Artefacts are the set behind **Hoarder** ("Collect all Relics of the Present and the Past"). The long name is easy to mistake for a chapter or a story objective, so keep it on its own sheet. With 37 entries it is the largest pickup set, and the per-chapter table above shows where they cluster: six in Chapter 3, five in Chapter 5, four each in Chapters 7 and 10.

Finishing the campaign does not tidy this list for you. **A new beginning** only asks you to complete all chapters; it says nothing about relics. Finish the story, then do a separate cleanup pass with the [Story Select cleanup guide](/story-select-cleanup/).

## Theseus' Echoes

Theseus' Echoes are the five Pierced Coins inside the Charm count. They are separate from the Blades and from the Relics even where the names sound alike, so do not tick one list off because a pickup looked similar to another.

## The one that Story Select cannot fix

Blades, Artefacts and Charms all count in **Story Select** after the credits, and quitting to the main menu makes a late pickup register immediately. The chapter menu even shows trackers for them.

**Resonance Points do not.**

- They save only when you reach the next checkpoint. Die first and you collect them again.
- They do not carry through Story Select, and reloading an earlier chapter resets your total to what it was then.
- There is no tracker for them in the chapter menu.
- You cannot farm them on a replay.

With 200 in the game and 165 needed for every skill and mastery, the margin is **35 points**. Sweep dead ends as you go; do not plan to come back.

## Where to check your progress

- **Artefacts menu**: relics, plus your Resonance Point total in the bottom-left corner.
- **Sophia menu**, below your skills: Blades and Charms.
- **Profile select on boot**: overall completion.

## What 100% looks like

Full completion means all four counters plus all **36** Steam achievements. The unlock rates show how few players get there. In the September 1, 2026 snapshot the collect-alls read **Plunderer 9.0%**, **In the name of my kin 4.3%**, **Superstitious 3.4%** and **Hoarder 3.2%**, with story completion at **26.3%**. By October 17 Plunderer had risen to **17.7%**, the Echoes to **9.5%**, Like a warrior to **11.5%**, and **A new beginning** to **60.5%**, so around four in ten Steam owners still had not finished the campaign.

Combat challenges are a separate track from exploration. **Inherited brutality** (four kills at once with a Focus attack) read **4.8%** on September 1. Plan a story run first and a 100% run second; the full achievement list, with requirements and rates, is on the [achievements guide](/achievements-guide/).

## Two exploration achievements that are not collectibles

- **Sea you later!** (32.0%): say hello to the turtle.
- **Theater enthusiast** (24.5%): watch the entire puppet show at the carnival in Chapter 2.

Both are missed by walking past, not by difficulty. The [missable achievements guide](/missable-achievements-guide/) covers them with the other one-off unlocks.

The bug-fix update names a **Chapter III** failure: Leni's lens interaction could not be triggered. If you left that room because nothing happened, the trigger is the thing to retest, not a chest you missed in a hallway. Install the update, load the room, and try the lens again before you replay the chapter from the start.

**Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy** launched **August 27, 2026**. On **October 20, 2026** it was **$49.99**, Very Positive at **5,676** of **6,117**. A stuck interaction in a well-reviewed game is still a stuck interaction. The notes marked this one fixed. A launch install from August will still show the old failure.

## What the note does and does not say

It says the lens interaction with Leni in Chapter III might not trigger, and that the case is fixed. It does not print a step-by-step puzzle solution, a reward, or a map pin. This page will not invent the beam path. The practical guide is the install order.

1. Confirm the bug-fix update is the build you are on. The October list is the long one.
2. Load a save that is still in the Chapter III room, or just before it. Do not skip ahead and hope a flag repairs itself. The notes do not promise a retroactive trigger.
3. Try the lens interaction again. If it fires, play the puzzle the game is now showing. Follow the prompt on screen.
4. If it still does not fire, you are either on the old build or looking at a new failure. One reload on the patched build is the test. Ten reloads on the launch build will reproduce the bug they closed.

The light puzzle is still the Minoan sphere's job. A lens that would not start is not proof the sphere system changed. It is proof one interaction in one chapter failed to hook. Once it hooks, the parry and the sling are the same tools as the rest of the island.

## Do not confuse it with the other chapter lines

Chapter IX could refuse a crouch in a petrified palace area after you backtracked. That is a crouch bug, not a lens bug. If you are in the palace and the problem is crouch, you are not in Chapter III.

Chapter XIII had a corrupted squeeze-through that could block a save. Chapter XIV's book could be unusable after the credits. Chapter 4 of a different game is not this chapter. Read the chapter number. A lens, a crouch, a save, and a book are four lines in one update. Fix the line you are standing in.

The Falchion could disappear after a DLC install. A missing sword will make every interaction feel broken, including a lens. Open the inventory. If the blade is gone, that is the DLC bug, marked fixed in the same list. Restore the weapon, then try the lens. Do not replay Chapter III because a sword icon vanished.

Enemies who ignored you after a restart were also fixed. If Leni's lens works and a guard stands still after you reload, that is the reaction bug, not the puzzle. Reload once on the patch. If they aggro, both fixes held.

## After the lens starts

Follow the interaction the game shows. Do not import a beam order from a video recorded on the launch build if that video's whole point was "the lens does nothing." That video is the bug. The patch is the opposite.

A shove off a ledge should now kill the enemy who used to survive it. Do not use a ledge kill as the puzzle solution unless the room asks for it. The lens is an interaction. The ledge is a combat fix. Same update, different rooms.

**36** achievements. **ACH_01** was **92.9%**. Nothing in the bug-fix post ties Leni's lens to a named rare pop. If the in-game list mentions the chapter, follow that list after the interaction works. Do not grind the room because a 9% achievement might be a lens. The **$49.99** game is worth the patch. The patch is worth one reload. Then leave the hallway and play the puzzle that finally starts.

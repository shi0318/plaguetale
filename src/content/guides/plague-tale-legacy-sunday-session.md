A recent file at 9.2 hours is a Sunday that had to be forced to stop. That is the length of this game, not a binge bug. The island run is about 10–13 hours for a story finish. People who 100% it sit closer to 15–21. Do not plan a week.

Combat is melee and parry, not a sling-first stealth check. If a camp-yard fight feels tedious, that is the mold break talking. Story Select exists so a Sunday can stop and resume a chapter, not so you can farm a missing rat swarm that is not in the game.

## How to spend the day

- Play for the look and the island, not for a collectible total. The [length page](/plague-tale-legacy-13-chapters-length/) is the clock.
- If a fight hitching on the coast is the reason you quit, install the [bugfix patch](/plague-tale-legacy-pssr-patch/) before you drop settings.
- Cleanup after credits is Story Select, not a second full run. See [Story Select cleanup](/plague-tale-legacy-story-select-cleanup/).
- Leni is the named friend in the release post. She is not a second playable Sunday. See [Leni](/characters/leni/).

If the first two games are the ones you loved, this Sunday is still Sophia’s island, not Amicia’s rats. The Very Positive pile from first-timers is the one that matches a new file.

**Blades from Heroes’ Graves** is a named objective in the released Steam achievement record for **A Plague Tale Legacy**. It belongs on a completion checklist, but the achievement record does not itself publish a coordinate list or a complete chapter-by-chapter route.

## How to track the objective without missing a return

Keep three fields in your notes: the chapter or scene name shown in-game, the landmark near the interaction, and whether the item was collected before leaving. If a route branches, mark the branch immediately instead of relying on a screenshot you may not recognize later.

Do not treat a missed collectible as a reason to restart the whole story until the chapter-select and cleanup behaviour has been checked on your build. The game has a separate achievement for completing all chapters, so an item cleanup route should be tracked separately from the story-completion route.

## Completion warning

The achievement name confirms the goal, not every location. This page does not invent coordinates, chapter numbers or reward values. Use the [collectibles hub](/collectibles/) and [missed artifacts guide](/find-missed-artifacts/) to record a verified location as you revisit it.

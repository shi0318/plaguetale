**Story spoilers for Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy, including Chapters 5 and 13.** Asobo’s November 26 lore Q&A confirms that the blood transferred to young Sophia carries the Macula and came from someone other than Asterion. The same Q&A confirms that multiple carriers can live at the same time and that Sophia experiences Ariadne through visions. Use those answers to read the convent scene, the Labyrinth and the visions as three pieces of the story. ## Whose blood was transferred to Sophia?

The developers confirm two facts about the opening scene. The blood carries the Macula. Asterion was not the donor. When you revisit the convent scene after the ending, keep the immediate event in view. Someone transfers blood into Sophia’s arm. The scene establishes contact with the Macula, and the November answer narrows what that blood can tell you about the person it came from. [November lore Q&A](https://community.focus-entmt.com/focus-entertainment/a-plague-tale-series/blogs/481-blog-lore-questions-from-the-fans)

The writers say other carriers existed and that some survived only briefly. They leave the donor unnamed in this answer. If you are building a timeline, put **unidentified donor** in that slot. The fact that a familiar carrier appears elsewhere in the series is insufficient to identify the donor, even if that person could overlap with the relevant period. A chronology can rule possibilities out, but it cannot name someone when the scene and the authors provide no identity.

## How long did Asterion spend in the Labyrinth?

Minos imprisoned Asterion when his powers appeared. Four years passed before Ariadne took part in the games. That interval runs from the imprisonment to her participation in the ancient period. The much longer span in the Q&A ends when Sophia comes to free him. The Macula kept him alive and frozen at a young age over the intervening centuries. Keep the short interval and the long interval separate if you are trying to place the flashbacks beside Sophia’s journey. [November lore Q&A](https://community.focus-entmt.com/focus-entertainment/a-plague-tale-series/blogs/481-blog-lore-questions-from-the-fans)

The Q&A also explains why there are petrified people in Chapter 13. It describes a mixture of warriors who failed in the Labyrinth and Minoan people who were trapped there. That distinction gives context to the bodies when you reach its depths. A single statue still needs its own evidence before you attach a name to it. For progression and the encounter, use the [Chapter 13 walkthrough](/walkthrough/chapter-13-as-one/) or the [Asterion character and boss guide](/characters/asterion/).

## Can more than one Macula carrier be alive at once?

Yes. The authors confirm the possibility in response to a question about whether Asterion overlapped with Basilius. You can place both stories on the same timeline without treating the Macula as a single position that had to pass from one person to the next. The answer confirms that living carriers can overlap. It leaves any meeting or direct transfer between the two undocumented. [November lore Q&A](https://community.focus-entmt.com/focus-entertainment/a-plague-tale-series/blogs/481-blog-lore-questions-from-the-fans)

This also gives you a way to check claims about a shared manifestation. The authors describe the Macula as appearing differently across people and periods, and say that what it expresses can reflect a carrier’s trauma and circumstances. They describe the Creature’s breathing-based perception as a design idea for an ancient being. If you need a route through an encounter, consult the [stealth and combat guide](/stealth-or-combat/) for the game’s visible cues.

## Why does Sophia experience Ariadne’s story?

Asobo says the connection comes through Sophia’s visions, which are intense enough for her to feel as if she is Ariadne. That is why the game lets you take control of Ariadne. When a playable sequence shifts to the Minoan era, you experience that character’s actions from the inside. The blood answer tells you something else about the present-day character. The vision explains your change in viewpoint and leaves the blood donor unidentified. [November lore Q&A](https://community.focus-entmt.com/focus-entertainment/a-plague-tale-series/blogs/481-blog-lore-questions-from-the-fans)

Ariadne is Asterion’s sister and his protector, a relationship the writers compare with Amicia and Hugo. To make sense of her decisions, ask what she was trying to do for Asterion while Minos was using his imprisonment for the games. The [Ariadne character guide](/characters/ariadne/) covers her role during the King Minos encounter, while the [series story guide](/story/) places Sophia’s adventure in relation to *A Plague Tale: Requiem*.

## When the fresco tells a different story

The Q&A identifies the Chapter 5 fresco as a depiction of the second trial in the staged city. In their answer about whether Asterion destroyed the island, the developers attribute the story to Minos. If you are revisiting that chapter to understand the scene, look at what the trial asks you to do and consider what Minos gains from a public story about Asterion. The fresco shows the version attached to the games. For the underlying legend and where Asobo departs from it, see the [Minotaur myth guide](/minotaur-myth-explained/).

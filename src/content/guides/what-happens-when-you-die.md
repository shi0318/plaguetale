A death in a street fight is a reload, not a build reset and not a collectible grant. The parry you missed is still the parry. Spend the next attempt on that swing, not on a new mastery.

Skill points after death and collectibles after death already have their own pages. This page only stops the panic.

## Next attempt

1. Parry the guard you can see. Do not open with the sling. [Street fight](/resonance-chapter-street-fight/).
2. If the room was dark, light it before the swing. [Night order](/resonance-night-room-order/).
3. If the death was a stuck prompt, install the October 6 update and retest that save. Do not start a new file first.
4. Read [skill points after death](/skill-points-after-death/) before you assume a death refunded a node.

There is no rat swarm to kite, and no difficulty menu that removes the parry. Dying does not unlock one.

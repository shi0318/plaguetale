The **Untouchable** achievement in **A Plague Tale Legacy** asks you to **successfully block six attacks in a row**. It is a timing challenge, not a kill-count objective.

## A safe way to set it up

Find an encounter with one predictable attacker and enough space to reset your position. Do not add a second target until the first six blocks are consistent. Keep your attention on the attack animation, not the enemy’s health bar, and avoid using a counterattack that ends the encounter before the sixth block.

If the group becomes chaotic, disengage and restart the attempt. A failed block resets the practical sequence, so forcing the seventh input after a mistake only creates another hit to recover from.

## What counts as a block

The achievement wording says “successfully block”; it does not require six kills, six parries or one specific chapter. Use the defensive input that the released build recognizes as a block, then confirm the achievement notification. Do not convert the requirement into a guessed frame window.

On **October 25, 2026**, the US Steam page listed Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy at **$49.99** with no discount shown. English reviews were **Very Positive**. The page counted **1,653** reviews for the game. In the last 30 days, **90%** of **497** reviews were positive.

That is the English store page. It is not an all-language total from earlier in October. Use it when you are looking at the button today. Do not average it with a different filter and call the change a surge. The label is Very Positive on both the recent slice and the longer English line. The price is still full.

The game has been out since **August 27, 2026**. The bug-fix update that restored 21:9, reduced Nemesis stutter, and fixed Leni's Chapter III lens is the build to be on before you treat 90% as a review of your install. A launch-day hitch is not what 497 recent reviews are all describing.

## What 90% of 497 means

Four hundred and ninety-seven reviews in 30 days is a solid month, not a 100-review wobble. Ninety percent positive, on a page that also calls the longer English record Very Positive at 1,653, is the clean reading. People are finishing the island and recommending it. A negative file about the story being less gripping than the earlier games can still be true. It is not the majority of this page.

It does not mean every interaction is fixed. Chapter III's lens, the Falchion after a DLC install, Focus Attack rebinds, and enemies who ignored you after a restart were named in the bug-fix list. If your complaint is one of those, update and retry once. A Very Positive month includes players who installed that list and players who did not.

It does not lower the disk requirement. **75 GB** on an SSD is still the store line. A 21:9 image on a hard drive can be the right aspect ratio and still hitch. Move the install. Then judge the month.

## Who should pay $49.99 today

Pay if you want Sophia's island, the parry, and the light puzzle, and a Very Positive English page is enough. The recent 90% does not promise you will love the story more than Requiem. It promises most English reviewers in that month recommended the game they played.

Wait if you need a sale. The October 25 page did not show one. Very Positive is not a coupon.

Update if you already own it and a lens or a bind feels dead. The percentage will not press the button for you. The patch will.

## A session that matches the page

1. Confirm the bug-fix update is installed.
2. Put the game on an SSD if it is not already. **75 GB** is the number.
3. Set 21:9 if that is your screen. Walk one known path. Judge the edges.
4. If Chapter III's lens never fired, load that room once on this build before you replay the chapter.
5. Then decide if **$49.99** is the island you wanted. The 497 reviews already decided for themselves.

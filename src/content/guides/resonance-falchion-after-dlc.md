If the **Falchion** disappeared after you installed the DLC, the bug-fix update already names that case. The notes say the weapon could vanish from inventory after the DLC install, and they list it as fixed. That is a better first check than an hour of backtracking through a chapter you already cleared.

**Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy** has been out since **August 27, 2026**. On **October 20, 2026** the Steam price was **$49.99**, no discount, and the review score was Very Positive: **5,676** of **6,117**. A missing sword in a well-reviewed game is still a missing sword. The patch is the place it was addressed.

## What to do before you replay a chapter

1. Confirm the bug-fix update is installed. A DLC drop on the launch build is the combination the note describes. The fix is in the later list, not in the DLC by itself.
2. Open the inventory on the current save, not on an older manual save from before the DLC. If the Falchion is back, the patch did the job. Equip it and fight one guard so you know the item is real and not a ghost icon.
3. If it is still missing, load a save from before the DLC install if you have one, update, then install or enable the DLC again. The notes do not promise every corrupted inventory will restore itself. They promise the disappearance bug is fixed going forward.
4. Do not buy a second copy of the weapon from a vendor you have not been told sells it. Nothing in the patch note says the Falchion moved to a shop. Searching a shop for a bug is how you spend the evening on the wrong problem.

The same update fixed several other "the game ate my input" cases that feel like missing items. Book navigation inputs could not be modified. The Focus Attack input could not be changed. The Prism unlock input could not be changed. Joystick swap preference was not saved after a relaunch. If the sword is present and the buttons feel wrong, rebind after the patch. The menu was the bug.

## Fights to retest once the sword is back

A missing weapon makes every combat complaint noisy. Once it is equipped, retest the lines the same update actually changed:

- An enemy pushed off an edge could survive. Shove one. If they die, stop treating ledges as random.
- An enemy could ignore you after a restart. Reload once with the Falchion equipped and confirm they still aggro.
- The "New skill available" popup could fail after Resonance points. Open the skill menu anyway.
- Chapter III: Leni's lens interaction could fail. If you left that room because nothing happened, the trigger is the thing to retest, not the sword.
- Chapter IX: crouch could fail in a petrified palace area after backtracking. Crouch is not the Falchion. Check it separately.
- Chapter XIII: a corrupted squeeze-through could block a save. If the sword vanished and the save also failed, you may be looking at two lines, not one curse.

NPC behavior and Nemesis navigation were improved in the same list. A pursuer taking a new line is not the Falchion bug. Do not unequip the sword to "fix" a pathing change.

## What the DLC install does not change

The price of the base game is still **$49.99** on the October 20 Steam listing. The Falchion fix is not a discount and not a new difficulty. Platforms in the official materials remain PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S. A PC inventory bug does not tell you the console copy lost the sword. Check the platform you are actually playing.

Minimum spec is **16 GB** of RAM, a 6 GB GTX 1060 or RX 5500 XT, and **75 GB** on an SSD. A missing weapon is not a VRAM problem. Do not lower the preset to find a sword. Lower the preset if the Nemesis door still hitches after the stutter line in this same update. Those are different fixes.

Steam lists **36** achievements. On October 20, **ACH_01** was **92.9%**. The thin saved sample included **ACH_22** at **9.2%**. No line in the bug-fix post says the Falchion is required for a named pop. If an achievement description in the game mentions the sword, follow that description after the item is back. Do not grind a chapter because a forum guessed the sword was the key.

## A short rule

Update. Open the inventory. Equip the Falchion if it returned. Retest one edge kill and one restart. Then go back to the fight you actually meant to learn.

The [flank parry](/how-to-parry-a-flank/) still starts by turning to face the second guard. A missing sword made that page impossible. A restored sword makes it ordinary again. The island did not change because a DLC install hid a blade. The inventory did, and the notes say that hide is fixed.

While you are in that inventory, check the other menu bugs from the same list so you do not "lose" the sword a second time by failing to bind it. Focus Attack, Prism unlock, and book navigation could all refuse a rebind before this patch. A Falchion on the old button, with Focus Attack stuck on a key you no longer press, feels exactly like a missing weapon in a fight. Rebind one control, swing once, then rebind the next. A stack of new binds plus a restored sword is how you convince yourself the patch stole the blade again.

Chapter XIV's book, which the notes say was unusable after the credits, is a different object. Do not spend the Falchion search in the post-credits book menu. The book bug is a book bug. The sword bug is an inventory bug after a DLC install. Same update, different lines. Fix the line you are actually on.

If the sword returns and the enemies still ignore you after a restart, that is the reaction bug, also marked fixed, and it is not a reason to unequip. Reload once on the patched build. If they aggro, both fixes held. If they do not, you have a new report, and the notes already ask players to keep sending the reports that made this list possible. Very Positive at **5,676** of **6,117** does not mean every inventory flag is perfect. It means the island is worth the patch. Install it, open the bag, and swing.

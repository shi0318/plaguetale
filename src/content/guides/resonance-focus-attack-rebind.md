The bug-fix update fixed three control locks. You could not modify book navigation inputs. You could not change the **Focus Attack** input. You could not change the **Prism** unlock input. If the parry felt wrong after you rebound the pad, the menu was the bug. Rebind Focus Attack on the patched build, swing once, and only then judge the fight.

**Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy** has been out since **August 27, 2026**. On **October 20** it was **$49.99**, Very Positive at **5,676** of **6,117**. A rebound that does not stick will make a good combat system look random. The notes say the stick is fixed. A launch install will still forget.

## Rebind one thing

1. Install the bug-fix update. The control lines are in that list.
2. Change Focus Attack only. Leave parry, sling, and crouch where they are until this one sticks.
3. Leave the menu. Hit the new button on one guard. If the focus attack fires, the line held.
4. Then change Prism unlock, if you need it on another button. Test that the same way.
5. Book navigation is the third. Change it only if the book is the tool you actually open. Chapter XIV's book had a separate "unusable after credits" bug. Do not rebind the book to fix a post-credits flag.

Joystick swap preference was also not saved after a relaunch. If the hands feel reversed after a reboot, check that preference before you rebind five buttons. A swap that resets is not a Focus Attack failure. Relaunch once after you set it. If it held, stop touching it.

## What a working bind does not change

The flank rule is still turn, then parry. A second guard is not a stealth fail. Focus Attack on a new button does not replace facing the body you mean to hit. If you rebound Focus Attack onto the parry button, you have made the menu bug worse. Give them different inputs. Test each.

Story mode and accessibility options are not this fix. A button that does not fire is not a damage problem. Do not drop difficulty because a rebind failed on the old build. Update, bind, swing.

The Falchion disappearing after a DLC install can feel like a dead button. Open the inventory before you blame Focus Attack. A missing sword on the old button, with Focus Attack stuck on a key you no longer press, is two bugs wearing one complaint. The update marks both fixed. Check the bag, then the bind.

Enemies who survived a ledge shove, and enemies who ignored you after a restart, are combat fixes in the same list. A working Focus Attack will not make a non-reacting guard flinch if you are still on the launch build. One update covers the menu and the restart. Install it once. Do not install it twice in your head and skip the file.

## After the button works

Fight one street. Parry the guard you can see. Spend Focus Attack on the recovery you meant to spend it on, not on the first idle body, until you know the new button. Then leave the menu alone. A stack of new binds plus a restored sword is how players convince themselves the patch stole the blade again.

Minimum spec is still **16 GB** of RAM, a 6 GB GTX 1060 or RX 5500 XT, and **75 GB** on an SSD. A rebind is not a VRAM problem. Do not lower the preset to find a button. Lower the preset if a Nemesis door still hitches after the stutter line in this same update.

**36** achievements. **ACH_01** at **92.9%** does not require a custom Focus Attack key. The rare pops near **9%** might mention a combat action. Read the in-game text after the button works. Do not grind a rebind screen. The **$49.99** fight is on the other side of one confirmed swing.

The numbers on this page were checked against the October 20, 2026 Steam listing, not against a launch-week memory. Price, review count, achievement total, and the patch lines named above are the facts to plan a session around. A thumbnail that contradicts them is a thumbnail. Play the build you have. If the machine is under the minimum spec printed on the store page, fix that before you fix the tactic. An SSD requirement is a disk requirement. A RAM number is a RAM number. A driver popup is a driver popup. None of those become optional because a guide got longer. The tactic still matters after the machine holds a frame. It does not matter during a hitch you mistook for an enemy tell.

Stop when the one test in the list above has a yes or a no. A second system, a second cosmetic, and a second difficulty change in the same minute will make the yes useless. Come back for the second change after the first one is written down. The rest of the site can wait until that note exists.

One confirmed swing ends the menu session. Focus Attack on a new button, Prism on its own button, book navigation only if you open the book. Joystick swap is the relaunch check, not a fifth rebind. A missing Falchion is an inventory check before any of those. The flank is still a turn and a parry after the button works. Players who stack five new binds and then miss a guard will blame the patch. The patch asked for one input to stick. Give it that. Then fight the street you came here to fight.

Leave the control screen after that one swing. The street, the flank, and the sling are the game. A menu that finally saves a bind has done its job.

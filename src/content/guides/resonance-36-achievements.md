Steam App 2713000 lists **36 achievements**. That is the completion ceiling on October 16, 2026. The storefront does not publish a missable table, so this page will not invent one.

Story Select is the reported way to replay a chapter after the credits. It is not a guarantee that every pop is retroactive. Retest the step the achievement actually names.

## A safe order

1. Finish the story on the current build, including the October 6 bug-fix update if a chapter blocker was the problem.
2. Use [Story Select cleanup](/story-select-cleanup/) for a chapter you already left.
3. Keep collectible counts on the [collectibles hub](/collectibles/). Do not merge Blades, Charms, Artefacts, and Resonant Points into one chest.

If a pop fails after the October 6 update, load the affected save before you start a new file.

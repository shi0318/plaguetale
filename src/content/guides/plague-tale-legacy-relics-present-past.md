**Relics of the Present and the Past** is a separate named objective in **A Plague Tale Legacy**. Its long title makes it particularly easy to confuse with a story chapter or another collectible group, so keep it on its own completion sheet.

## A reliable cleanup sheet

Use the chapter or scene label shown by the game, a landmark, the interaction type, and a collected/not-collected box. If two relics are in the same area, add the order in which you reached them. That makes a partial run recoverable without claiming a precise coordinate that has not been checked.

The public achievement record confirms the objective name, but it does not publish a complete location table. A location guide should be expanded only when an in-game route has been confirmed on the released build.

## Pair it with chapter completion

The achievement **A new beginning** requires completing all Chapters. That is a story goal, not proof that every relic was collected. Finish the narrative, then use the [find missed artifacts guide](/find-missed-artifacts/) and the [collectibles hub](/collectibles/) for a separate cleanup pass.

Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy has been **Very Positive on Steam** at every check since its **August 27, 2026** launch, and it sat at **84 on Metacritic** in October. This page keeps each dated snapshot in one place, then summarises what reviewers praise, what they push back on, and how to read the numbers before you buy.

## Is it well reviewed?

Yes. Asobo Studio's prequel, published by Focus Entertainment, has held a **Very Positive** Steam label from the first day after launch through late October 2026. Metacritic listed it at **84** on October 12, 2026. Neither score says it beats *Requiem*; reviewers mostly say this combat and this island hold up for the length they last.

## The last 30 days

The recent-reviews slice is the best guide to how the patched game is landing. On October 25, **90% of 497** English reviews from the previous 30 days were positive; on October 26 it was **90% of 475**. Close to 500 reviews in a month is a steady stream, not a launch-week spike, and 90% is a clear recommendation rather than a split.

That month falls after the October 6 bug-fix update, but it does not prove every reviewer installed it. A stuck Chapter III lens, a missing Falchion after a DLC install, a Focus Attack binding that would not change, or a hitch at a Nemesis door can all be the launch build. If one of those is your complaint, update and check once before adding your own review.

## What players praise

Across the snapshots, the praise clusters in the same places:

- **The look and the music.** Reviewers repeatedly single out the visuals and the score.
- **A story that ends when it should.** Players describe a linear story that holds for roughly ten hours rather than padding itself out.
- **Combat.** Reviews call the melee exciting and say it lands better than worried previews suggested.
- **Stability.** In September, some reviewers called it shockingly stable next to earlier Plague Tale PC launches.
- **The Beast.** The creature hunting Sophia is described as more frightening than the series' rats.

A few September reviews went further and called it a personal 2026 game-of-the-year pick so far. Longer reviews, from players at 11–16 hours, describe it as a Tomb Raider / Uncharted mix set in the Plague Tale world.

## What players criticise

The complaint that survives every snapshot is the **mold break**: no rat swarms, more melee, and characters who are not the pair players already love. Players who finished *Innocence* and *Requiem* often say those two remain the better games — and then recommend this one anyway as its own thing. If that is your complaint, the score will not convert you. If you are new to the series, first-timer reviews are the ones that match your run.

Early reviews also mentioned friction with traversal interactions. Later fixes arrived in the [October 6 bug-fix update](/resonance-plague-tale-legacy-october-6-update/), which is the build to be on before you judge stutter or a stuck chapter yourself.

## What early reviews reported about the game

The first-week reviews were also a source of practical detail. Players reported about **10 hours on Normal**, **13 chapters**, a **Story Select** menu for replaying chapters, a linear story, and a blend of puzzles, exploration, combat, and stealth. Some reported that collectibles persist through death while skill points do not. Those details now have their own pages: the [length guide](/how-long-to-beat/), the [Story Select cleanup guide](/story-select-cleanup/), and [stealth or combat](/stealth-or-combat/).

## How to read the score before buying

A Very Positive label tells you how the reviewing sample feels, not how your PC will run the game or how long your run will take. A few practical rules:

- **Use the latest dated snapshot.** Do not paste an older Metacritic figure next to a newer Steam count and call both "current."
- **Do not turn a review count into a length estimate.** The [length guide](/how-long-to-beat/) is the clock; 2,033 positive reviews is not a 30-hour game.
- **Price does not change the shape of the game.** October checks showed **$49.99** with no discount until a later US check on October 26 found **$39.99**. A sale changes whether about ten hours at that price feels fair to you; it does not add rats or make it longer.
- **Separate a bug from a verdict.** If your complaint is stutter near a Nemesis area or a chapter blocker, install the October 6 update before deciding the reviews were wrong.

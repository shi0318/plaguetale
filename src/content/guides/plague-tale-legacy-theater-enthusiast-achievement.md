The **Theater enthusiast** achievement in **A Plague Tale Legacy** requires you to **watch a carnival puppet show to the end**. It is an optional observation objective, so leaving as soon as the scene begins can miss the trigger.

## The safe attempt

When you reach the carnival puppet show, remain in the viewing area and let the performance finish. Do not skip the scene, leave early, or trigger the next objective while the puppets are still moving. If the achievement does not appear, reload the scene and watch the entire performance without opening another interaction.

The requirement confirms the action and the completion point, but not a universal chapter number in the public list. Keep the location note tied to the in-game scene name on your build instead of copying an unverified chapter label.

Pair this with the [achievements guide](/guides/achievements-guide/) and [chapter story guide](/guides/story/). It is separate from the 36-achievement total and from **A new beginning**, which requires completing all Chapters.

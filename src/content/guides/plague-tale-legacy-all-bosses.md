Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy has **six real boss fights** across its 14 chapters. Three are fought in Theseus's memories, three in Sophia's own time. None of them is a damage race: every one is decided by whether you read the attack colour correctly.

## The colour rule

| Attack | Tell | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Normal swing | No glow | Parry — fills the stun bar |
| Yellow attack | Yellow flash | Parry for a counter; **Echo's Return** can throw the attacker |
| Red attack | Red flash | **Dodge**, unless you bought the Aegean Deflect mastery that lets you parry red |

Every boss below is a different mix of those three. Buy **Aegean Deflect** early — the mastery that lets Sophia parry red attacks is the single biggest change to the back half of the game.

## 1. Cadmos — Chapter 5, Desolation It Wrought

The first arena boss, fought as Theseus after a gauntlet of competing warriors. He is the tutorial for the rhythm the rest of the game uses: parry the normal string, step out of the red, punish the recovery. Theseus has his own Focus Attack, and Sophia's upgrades do not carry into his sections — do not expect your skill tree here.

## 2. Cercyon — Chapter 7, Death It Sowed

A sword-and-shield champion at the end of the Black Temple arena. Two things catch people out:

- **His health bar resets once.** The fight is not bugged; you are in phase two.
- Phase two leans much harder on red attacks. Stop trying to parry everything and start dodging.

Land one or two hits, then reset your guard. Over-committing after a parry is what kills most runs here.

## 3. Faro — Chapter 9, Night Has Come

The first boss in Sophia's own era, in the palace courtyard, and the fastest human in the game. He throws long combo chains that interrupt your attacks if you swing into them.

- Let the combo finish, then parry or dodge the **last** hit and answer with a Critical Strike.
- You must drain his health **twice**.
- This is the fight the red-parry mastery pays for.

Sophia refuses the killing blow, and Faro joins her for the next two chapters.

## 4. Irene — Chapter 11, Patera

The traitor, fought after her dagger finds Faro at the mine exit. Three stages:

1. Heavy on red attacks — parry them with the mastery, or keep your distance and wait out the string.
2. She vanishes and throws daggers from the dark. The thrown dagger is a **yellow** attack: parry it.
3. More of the same until the last health bar empties.

If your timing is off, back away. Chasing Irene is how the fight goes wrong.

## 5. King Minos — Chapter 12, Fading Light

Fought **as Ariadne**, not Sophia, inside Theseus's memory — and Ariadne cannot parry red attacks at all.

- **Phase 1:** almost everything is red. Dodge, then punish the heavy axe swing while it is stuck in the ground.
- **Phase 2:** the axe catches fire and leaves burning ground. Keep moving, never fight in a fire trail, and get behind him after a heavy swing.

## 6. Asterion, the Minotaur — Chapter 13, As One

The final boss, and a mechanics fight rather than a duel. You stand before the Minotaur statue at the heart of the Labyrinth:

1. Break the cracked heart with the **Grapnel**.
2. Take the light-beam weapon from the device at the far end and shoot the heart.
3. Wounds open on the statue and creatures crawl out — burn them with the beam.
4. When Ariadne tells you to run, zipline to the raised platform and avoid the corruption slams.
5. Grapnel the mouth from both platforms to break it open.
6. After the escape, climb the circular staircase and finish the wounds with the beam.

Nothing here is parried. Keep moving, keep the beam on the wounds, and treat the creatures as a timer rather than an enemy list.

## What is not a boss

The creature that hunts Sophia from Chapter 6 onward **cannot be killed**. Every encounter with it is a chase or a stealth section: stay in the light, use braziers, and never leave a blue trail by sprinting in the dark.

**Charms** are a named collection objective in the released **A Plague Tale Legacy** achievement data. They should be tracked separately from Blades from Heroes’ Graves, Theseus’ Echoes and the Relics of the Present and the Past.

## How to make a Charm checklist

Create one row for the scene, the nearest puzzle or landmark, the interaction state and the next exit. A note such as “near the light puzzle after the water crossing” is more useful than a page number with no in-game reference. Add a screenshot only after confirming the same object on a second load.

## Do not merge collection categories

The achievement names are evidence that these objectives exist; they are not evidence that they use one shared counter. If an in-game tracker is visible, copy its wording exactly. If it is not, keep the categories separate until the completion condition is tested.

The [collectibles hub](/collectibles/) is the correct place to connect this page with verified location notes. This page deliberately leaves exact coordinates open instead of turning a guessed route into a false answer.

Sophia has **six skills**, each with **two masteries**, plus a free Focus Attack she gets from the story. Unlocking everything costs **165 Resonance Points** out of the **200** in the game, so you have a 35-point buffer — but only if you do not waste points early.

## The six skills

| Skill | What it does | Masteries |
|---|---|---|
| **Aegean Deflect** | A last-moment parry deals double stun damage | **Parry red attacks**; extra sword damage after a deflect |
| **Hero's Step** | Double-tap dodge for a second chained dodge | Shorter recovery between dodges; longer invulnerability on the second |
| **Heroic Surge** | A dagger critical kill grants an extra health point that soaks the next hit | Longer duration; wider trigger conditions |
| **Echo's Return** | Grab a normal or yellow attacker and throw them into the group, stunning everyone | Two upgrade paths, including red-attack handling |
| **Bullbreaker Kick** | The second kick pushes even an unstunned enemy | More reliable push on heavies; bonus stun on collisions |
| **Fated Cast** | Store a picked-up throwable on your belt and throw it later | Makes throwables a planned tool instead of a pickup |

Each new skill branch costs more than the last, so the order you buy in decides how much of the game you spend under-equipped.

## The unlock order that works

1. **Aegean Deflect → the red-attack parry mastery.** Buy this first and buy the mastery as soon as you can afford it. Every boss from Faro (Chapter 9) onward throws red attacks in volume, and Irene in Chapter 11 is built around them. This one purchase changes more fights than the other five skills combined.
2. **Bullbreaker Kick.** Shielded soldiers and Brutes are the standard camp encounter. The kick breaks guard; the collision mastery turns a crowded room into free stun damage.
3. **Hero's Step.** Two chained dodges with invulnerability is how you survive King Minos in Chapter 12, where Ariadne cannot parry red at all.
4. **Echo's Return.** Worth it for **Skilled maneuverer** — stun three enemies at once — which 34.3% of Steam players have. Save it for a three-enemy camp rather than hunting it.
5. **Heroic Surge.** Good, not urgent. The extra health point rewards a playstyle you will already have by the late chapters.
6. **Fated Cast.** Last. Throwables are useful but situational, and by the time you can afford this you have the points to spare.

## Focus Attack is free

Focus unlocks through the story, not with points. The gauge fills by attacking, parrying and dodging; when it is full Sophia sweeps everything around her. It is also the escape from a Petrified grab in the late chapters, so do not spend it the moment it fills. **Pierced Coins** give a moderate chance not to empty the gauge when you use it.

Theseus and Ariadne have their own Focus Attacks in the memory sections, and none of Sophia's purchases carry over.

## Charms stack

Charms are passive — there is no loadout screen, and duplicates stack:

- **Beads** — higher chance an archer's arrow misses.
- **Trinkets** — chance to survive an otherwise lethal hit.
- **Pierced Coins** — chance not to empty the Focus gauge.
- **Alec's Pendant** (Heritage Pack) — boosts all your Charms' stats.

## Resonance Points: the one real trap

Points only save when you reach the **next checkpoint**. Die before it and you collect them again. They do **not** carry through Story Select, and reloading an earlier chapter resets you to the total you had then. You cannot farm them on a replay. With 200 in the game and 165 needed for **Like a warrior**, you can miss up to 35 — but not 50.
